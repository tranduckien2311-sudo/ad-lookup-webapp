EFFECTIVITY AIRBUS A330 aircraft models -301, -321, -322, -323, -341, -342 and -343, all serial numbers, except those E on which AIRBUS modification 41652 has been embodied in production or AIRBUS Service Bulletin (SB) A330-53-3127 Revision 01 has been embodied in service. S 2. REASONS: During the fatigue tests (EF2) of the fuselage, initiation and development of cracks were evidenced at the circumferential joint of frame 53.3. R This situation, if not corrected, could lead to a reduction in the structural integrity of the fuselage. E This new

Airworthiness Directive (AD): - takes up the requirements of AD 2002-529, P - renders mandatory the reduction of the modification embodiment threshold, and - introduces a new revision of this modification via Revision 01 of SB A330-53-3127 further to embodiment difficulties encountered in service. U 3. MANDATORY ACTIONS AND COMPLIANCE TIMES: S The following measures are rendered mandatory from November 09, 2002 [on the effective date of this AD irworthiness Directive2002-529]. Unless already accomplished, Before accumulation of 14,7900 flight cycles (FC) or 512,4100 flight hours (FH) since the first flight of the aircraft, whichever occurs first, reinforce the structure of the center fuselage at the upper circumferential joint of frame 53.3 (between LH stringer 13 and RH stringer 13), in accordance with the instructions given in SB A330-53-3127, Revision 01. …/… n/CG
