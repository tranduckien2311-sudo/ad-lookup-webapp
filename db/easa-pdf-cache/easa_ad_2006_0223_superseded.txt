D Type/Model designation(s) : Type Approval Holder’s Name : AIRBUS A318, A319, A320 and A321 aircraft E TCDS Number : EASA A.064 D Foreign AD : None Supersedure : DGAC F-2005-051, EASA approval No. 2005-2572 E

ATA 27 Flight controls – Trimmable Horizontal Stabilizer Actuator S R Manufacturer: AIRBUS (formerly AIRBUS INDUSTRIE)

Applicability: AIRBUS A318, A319, A320 and A321 all certified models, all E manufactured serial numbers.

Reason: The Trimmable Horizontal Stabilizer Actuator (THSA) of the Single Aisle aircraft has been tested on rig in order to check its behaviour in P case of primary load path failure. In that configuration, the loads are transferred to the secondary nut, which should jam, preventing any THS motion. U Tests have shown that the secondary nut did not jam as expected, preventing the detection of the primary load path failure. In order to verify the integrity of the THS actuator primary load path, S DGAC

Airworthiness Directive (AD) F-2005-051 mandated two repetitive inspections allowing confirming that the secondary load path is not loaded. Further tests performed on the secondary load path highlighted the need to reduce the interval for the THSA upper attachment inspection to 10 months.

Effective Date: 7 August 2006 1/2
