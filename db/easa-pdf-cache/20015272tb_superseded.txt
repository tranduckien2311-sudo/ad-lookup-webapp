D AIRBUS A330 aircraft E THSA - Operational life limit (ATA 27) D 1. APPLICABILITY: AIRBUS A330 aircraft, all certified models, all serial numbers. E 2. REASONS: The life limits of the aircraft flight controls actuators and in particular of the THSA given in the S Revision 8 of AMM Chapter 05-11-00 Configuration 1 (dated September 15, 1999) are not addressed by the definition of the structural life limits of Safe Life items as defined in Section 9.1 (Life limits/Monitored parts) of the Airworthiness Limitations Section (located in the MPD Section 9) which replaces the aircraft AMM Chapter 05-11. As a result these life limits are removed from the above R documents and integrated into this

Airworthiness Directive (AD). The operational life limits of the THSA P/N 47147-200 to 47147-350 are originally due to an excessive chrome wear on the screw which would lead to long term fatigue cracks if not corrected. E During the overhaul of these THSA an higher than anticipated No-Back ball bearing cage wear has been discovered which may lead to an aircraft critical situation if not corrected. The aim of this AD is to require the modification of the THSA when they have reached their P operational life limit, by AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin (SB) A330-27-3052. This SB consists in modifying the THSA by the supplier TRW, in particular to replace the No-Back ball bearings. In addition, this AD gives the temporary life limits of the last certified P/N. U The reason for Revision 1 of this AD was to clarify the life limits applicable to THSA P/N 47147-400, 47147-500 and 47172-300, depending on whether the THSA is installed as a new equipment, or whether it is installed through retrofit of an older standard. This differentiation has been introduced in S paragraph 3. The aim of this Revision 2 is to extend the life limits of the THSA P/N 47172 and 47172-300 according to the result of an endurance test and to specify calendar time for the modification of THSA P/N 47147-200 up to 47147-350, already mentioned in the CN 2002-414(B) R1. n/ CG …/… AIRBUS October 16, 2002 2001-527(B) R2 A330 aircraft
