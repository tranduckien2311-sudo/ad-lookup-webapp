EASA PAD No.: 26-129 Reason: Occurrences of electronic centralised aircraft monitor (ECAM) messages requiring flight control remote module (FCRM) replacement have been reported. Further investigation identified an issue linked to solder structural fatigue. This condition, if not corrected, could lead to failure of a flight control actuator, possibly resulting in reduced control of the aeroplane. To address this potential unsafe condition, Airbus issued the SB at original issue, to introduce a life limit for the affected parts and to provide instructions for determination of their accumulated FH and FC. Consequently, EASA issued AD 2025-0008 to require replacement of affected parts before exceeding the life limit. Since that AD was issued, it has been determined that the life limit must be reduced. The SB has been published to update the life limit of the affected parts. For the reason described above, this AD partially retains the requirements of the EASA AD 2025- 0008, which is superseded, and introduces reduced life limit for the affected parts. Required Action(s) and Compliance Time(s): Required as indicated by this AD, unless the action(s) required by this AD have been already accomplished: Part Replacement: (1) Within the compliance time as provided in Table 1 of this AD, replace each affected part with a serviceable part, as defined in this AD, in accordance with the instructions of the SB. Note 1: The FC and the FH referred in paragraph (1) of this AD are those accumulated by the affected parts since new (first installation on an aeroplane). If unknown, the SB provides instructions to determine the FC and FH accumulated by an affected part since new. Table 1 - Affected Parts Replacement Compliance Time: A or B, whichever occurs later Before the affected part accumulates 5 500 FC or 32 000 FH (see Note 1 of this AD), A whichever occurs first B Within 2 years and 5 months after the effective date of this AD Part(s) Installation: (2) From the effective date of this AD, it is allowed to install an affected part on any aeroplane, provided the part is a serviceable part, and that, following installation, the affected part is replaced as required by paragraph (1) of this AD (see Note 2 of this AD).
