AIRBUS INDUSTRIE D A330 aircraft Inspection and replacement of "SAMM" elevator servo-control (ATA 27) E APPLICABILITY: D AIRBUS INDUSTRIE A330 aircraft, models -202, -223, -243, -301, -321, -322, -323, -341, -342 and -343, all serial numbers, fitted with "SAMM" elevator servo-control (SC) with Part Number SC4800-2 to SC4800-7 or SC4800-8 except aircraft which have received in production AIRBUS INDUSTRIE E modification 47674 or in service AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin (SB) A330-27-3076 at initial issue or any further approved revision. S REASONS: 1.

Airworthiness Directive (AD) 2000-025-109(B) R1 was issued in order to prevent a rupture of active R elevator servo-control piston rod due to the degradation of the self lubricated liner from the eye end spherical bearing. In case of a rupture of the piston rod on both the active and damping mode servo-controls on the E same side, the elevator is no more damped and becomes uncontrollable. Note : For similar reasons, "SAMM" elevator servo-controls fitted with "FRANKENJURA" eye-ends have been subject to

Airworthiness Directive (AD) 98-539-088(B). P 2. This new AD restates the inspection of the SC concerned by AD 2000-025-109(B) R1 until their mandatory replacement with SC newly designed by the manufacturer at thresholds required by the paragraph Compliance B of this AD. The piston rod of these new SC is fitted with roller bearing eye U end. Consequently, this AD replaces AD 2000-025-109(B) R1. S COMPLIANCE: A.
