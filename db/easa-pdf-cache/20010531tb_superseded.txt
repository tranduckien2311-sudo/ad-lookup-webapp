Airworthiness Directive(s) replaced: Not applicable 2001-053 original issue D Person in charge of airworthiness: Type(s): AIRBUS A330 aircraft E Type certificate(s) No. 184 TCDS No 184 ATA chapter: Subject: D 52 Doors - Pax/crew and emergency exit doors - Girt bar slider mechanism 1. EFFECTIVITY: E AIRBUS A330 aircraft, models -201, -202, -203, -223, -243, -301, -321, -322, -323, -341, -342 and -343, all serial numbers. S Note 1: No additional work is required for operators having shown compliance to this

Airworthiness Directive (AD) at original issue. 2. REASONS: R This AD results from an escape slide deployment test performed on an A330 aircraft. The test has shown that a component of the slide release mechanism (slider) was found unserviceable E (spring function inoperative due to corrosion or missing). If not corrected, this defect may lead the slide to become detached from the door once inflated. The aim of this Revision 1 is to introduce MPD task 521000-07-1 for doors type A and MPD task 522200-09-1 P for doors type 1 as an equivalent means of compliance to repetitive inspection required by this AD. 3. MANDATORY ACTIONS AND COMPLIANCE TIMES: U Unless already accomplished, the following measures are rendered mandatory from the effective date of this AD at original issue: 3.1. Not later than 18 months in-service or within 550 flight hours following the effective date of this AD at S original issue, whichever occurs later, inspect and in case a slider is found without locking function, repair or replace before next flight in accordance with the instructions given in AIRBUS All Operator Telex (AOT) A330-52A3063 issued August 02, 2000. n/CG
