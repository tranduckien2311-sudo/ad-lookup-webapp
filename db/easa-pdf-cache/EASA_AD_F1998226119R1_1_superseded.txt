AIRBUS INDUSTRIE d A321 aircraft Automatic landing limitations (ATA 22) e d APPLICABILITY e AIRBUS INDUSTRIE A321 aircraft all certified models, all serial numbers, in the following configurations: s 1. Equipped with CFM engines with AIRBUS INDUSTRIE modification 25199 or Service Bulletin A320-22-1045. However, engines with AIRBUS INDUSTRIE modification 25469 or 26968 in r production or with Service Bulletin A320-22-1054 or A320-22-1064 in service, are not affected. 2. Equipped with IAE engines with AIRBUS INDUSTRIE modification 25200 or Service Bulletin e A320-22-1046. However, engines with AIRBUS INDUSTRIE modification 26243 or 26716 in production or Service Bulletin A320-22-1062 or A320-22-1065 in service, are not affected. p REASON Automatic landings performed on runways with a magnetic orientation between (and including values) u 170 degrees to 190 degrees could generate automatic roll out guidance problems due to software calculation errors.
