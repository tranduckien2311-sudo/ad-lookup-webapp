Airworthiness Directive. Translation of ‘Consigne de Navigabilité’ ref. : 92-198-027(B) In case of any difficulty, reference should be made to the French original issue. AIRBUS INDUSTRIE A320 Aircraft Fuselage - Inspection - Fasteners of skin panel to frame 15 junction The present

Airworthiness Directive applies to A320 AIRBUS INDUSTRIE aircraft MSN 005 to 012 included. In order to detect any damage related to structural fatigue phenomenon, the following measures are rendered mandatory on the effective date of this

Airworthiness Directive : 1/ Before accumulation of 10,000 flights, inspect fuselage fasteners of skin panel to frame 15 junction in accordance with instructions given in AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-53-1069. 2/ Repeat the inspections as defined in paragraph 1. of this

Airworthiness Directive in accordance with requirements as defined in AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-53-1069. Note : Total application of AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-53-1004 Revision 1 cancels requirements of this

Airworthiness Directive. Ref. : AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-53-1004 Revision 1 AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-53-1069 EFFECTIVE DATE : OCTOBER 10, 1992 n/C AIRBUS INDUSTRIE September 30, 1992 92-198-027(B) A320 Aircraft
