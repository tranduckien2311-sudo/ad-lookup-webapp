ATA 52 Doors – Forward Cargo Door Beam 3 Area – Inspection

Manufacturer(s): Airbus (formerly Airbus Industrie)

Applicability: Airbus A330-201, A330-202, A330-203, A330-223, A330-223F, A330-243, A330-243F, A330-301, A330-302, A330-303, A330-321, A330-322, A330-323, A330-341, A330-342 and A330-343 aeroplanes, all manufacturer serial numbers (MSN), except those on which Airbus modification 202702 has been embodied in production, and Airbus A340-211, A340-212, A340-213, A340-311, A340-312, A340-313, A340-541, A340-542, A340-642 and A340-643 aeroplanes, all MSN.

Reason: An A330 aeroplane operator reported recently cases of crack findings on two different aeroplanes, at frame 20A and at frame 20B close to beam 3 of the forward cargo door. The first finding was detected during scheduled maintenance, while the second one was found during an inspection prompted by the first finding. Subsequent analyses of these cracks identified that the first crack initiated at frame 20B, which is the first primary load path, leading to excessive loads at frame 20A and consequent cracking. Nevertheless, on the other aeroplane, a crack was detected on frame 20A only. Rupture of both frames 20A and 20B could lead to frame 21 failure after a limited number of flight cycles (FC). This condition, if not detected and corrected, may potentially result in the loss of structural integrity of the forward cargo door, which could ultimately jeopardise the aeroplane’s safe flight. For the reasons described above, this AD requires identification of the Part Number (P/N) of the affected forward cargo doors, a one-time detailed inspection (DET) of each affected door and, depending on findings, accomplishment of applicable corrective action(s). This AD is considered to be an interim action and further AD action may follow.

Effective Date: 03 November 2014 Required Action(s) Required as indicated, unless accomplished previously: and Compliance (1) Within 200 FC after the effective date of this AD, accomplish a DET of the Time(s): forward cargo door, having a P/N as listed in Table 1 of this AD, at frames 20A, 20B and 21 areas located above beam 3, from outside and inside in accordance with the instructions of Airbus AOT A52L010-14. Table 1 – Affected forward cargo door P/N F523-70500-000 F523-70500-010 F523-70550-002 F523-70500-004 F523-70500-012 F523-70550-004 F523-70500-006 F523-70500-014 F523-70550-008 F523-70500-008 F523-70550-000 F523-70550-050 (2) If, during the DET as required by paragraph (1) of this AD, a discrepancy is found, before next flight, contact Airbus for approved instructions (repair, additional NDT inspection and/or part replacement) and, within the compliance time specified in those instructions, accomplish those instructions accordingly. (3) Within 30 days after the accomplishment of a DET as required by paragraph (1) of this AD, report the results (including no findings) to Airbus. (4) From the effective date of this AD, it is allowed to install a forward cargo door having a P/N listed in Table 1 of this AD, provided that, prior to installation on an aeroplane, the door is inspected and, depending on findings, corrected in in accordance with the instructions of Airbus AOT A52L010-14.
