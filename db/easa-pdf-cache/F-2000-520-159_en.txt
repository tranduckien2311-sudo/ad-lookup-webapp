Airworthiness Directive applies to all AIRBUS INDUSTRIE A319/A320/A321 aircraft, all certified models, Manufacturer Serial Numbers (MSN) 0835, 0846, 0864, 0865, 0867, 0871, 0875, 0883, 0885, 0886, 0889, 0891, 0893, 0896, 0898, 0901, 0909, 0910, 0911, 0917, 0918, 0923, 0924, 0930, 0932, 0933, 0934, 0935, 0938, 0940, 0941, 0944, 0946, 0951, 0955, 0959, 0965, 0972, 0974, 0975, 0983, 0984, 0986, 0989, 0990, 0995, 0999, 1001, 1003, 1006, 1007, 1008, 1009, 1012, 1014, 1023, 1026, 1031, 1032, 1035 and 1037, without AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-55-1028. REASONS: This

Airworthiness Directive is prompted by recent findings of localized debondings of spar, stringer and rib to skin attachments on several Vertical Stabilizer Spar Boxes. The debonding results from bonding surface contamination during production process. This condition, if not corrected, could affect the structural integrity of the Vertical Stabilizer Spar Box.

Airworthiness Directive 2000-208-148(B) R1 described a one time inspection of the Vertical Stabilizer Spar Box. This new

Airworthiness Directive replaces the previous

Airworthiness Directive, and introduces a repetitive inspection and a modification. ACTIONS: 1. Within 1100 flights from the previous inspection performed in accordance with the

Airworthiness Directive 2000-208-148(B) R1, inspect the Vertical Stabilizer Spar Box in accordance with AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-55A1027 at Revision 1. - If no debondings or debondings within the allowable limits as described in the AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-55A1027 at Revision 1 are detected, repeat the inspection in accordance with AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-55A1027 at Revision 1 at intervals not exceeding 1100 flights. - In case debondings beyond the allowable limits as described in the AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-55A1027 at Revision 1 are found, repair the affected areas before further flight in accordance with the repair instructions detailed in the AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-55A1027 at Revision 1, and repeat the inspection in accordance with AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-55A1027 at Revision 1 at intervals not exceeding 1100 flights. n/JJD …/… AIRBUS INDUSTRIE December 13, 2000 2000-520-159(B) A319/A320/A321 aircraft
