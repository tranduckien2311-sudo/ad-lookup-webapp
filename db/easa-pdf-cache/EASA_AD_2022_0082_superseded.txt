New and/or more restrictive tasks: This includes all tasks that are new and all tasks for which a threshold or interval was reduced, which were introduced into the ALS (as defined in this AD) since the previous ALS Revision that is currently incorporated in the AMP.

Reason: The airworthiness limitations for Airbus A318, A319, A320 and A321 aeroplanes, which are approved by EASA, are currently defined and published in the Airbus A318/A319/A320/A321 ALS document(s). These instructions have been identified as mandatory for continued airworthiness. The Safe Life Airworthiness Limitations Items (SL - ALI) are specified in ALS Part 1. D Failure to accomplish these instructions could result in an unsafe condition. EASA previously issued AD 2020-0080 to require accomplishment of all SL - ALI maintenance tasks E as described in ALS Part 1 at Revision 07. Since that AD was issued, Airbus published the ALS, containing new and/or more restrictive maintenance tasks, and new A320 family models have been certified. D For the reason described above, this AD retains the requirements of EASA AD 2020-0080, which is superseded, expands the Applicability, and requires accomplishment of the actions specified in the E ALS.

Required Action(s) and Compliance Time(s): S Required as indicated, unless accomplished previously: Maintenance Tasks and Replacement of Life Limited Parts : R (1) From the effective date of this AD, replace each component before exceeding the applicable life limit, as specified in the ALS, as applicable to aeroplane model and depending on aeroplane configuration (see Note 1 of this AD). E Note 1: For the purpose of this AD, the thresholds as defined in the ‘Compliance Time’ pages of the ALS include specific compliance times for certain tasks. P AMP Revision: (2) Within 12 months after the effective date of this AD, revise the approved AMP by incorporating U the limitations described in the ALS, as applicable to aeroplane model and depending on aeroplane configuration. Credit: S (3) If, before the effective date of this AD, the AMP has been revised to incorporate the tasks and limitations as specified in a previous ALS variation or revision, that action ensures the continued accomplishment of those tasks and limitations.
