Design Approval Holder’s Name: Type/Model designation(s): SCHEMPP‐HIRTH FLUGZEUGBAU GmbH Ventus‐3M powered sailplanes Effective Date: 30 October 2026 TCDS Number(s): EASA.A.627 Foreign AD: Not applicable Supersedure: None ATA 24 – Electrical Power – Charge Controller – Modification Manufacturer(s): Schempp‐Hirth Flugzeugbau GmbH (Schempp-Hirth) Applicability: Ventus-3M powered sailplanes having serial numbers (s/n) 021 MP up to s/n 084 MP (inclusive), s/n 091 MP up to s/n 100 MP (inclusive), s/n 102 MP up to s/n 111 MP (inclusive), and s/n 115 MP up to s/n 125 MP (inclusive). Definitions: For the purpose of this AD, the following definitions apply: The TN: Schempp-Hirth Technical Note (TN) No. 627-16 Revision 1. Groups: Group 1 powered sailplanes are those on which the charge controller is installed without spacer sleeves, as described in Action 2 of the TN. Group 2 powered sailplanes are those that are not Group 1. Reason: It has been determined that if the engine of a powered sailplane is operated for an extended period at high ambient temperatures and with high charging current, the charge controller may fail, due to overheating. EASA AD No.: 2026-0197 This condition, if not corrected, could lead to damage of the electrical components of the engine control system, possibly resulting in engine in-flight shut-down and reduced control of the sailplane. To address this potential unsafe condition, Schempp-Hirth published the TN, providing instructions for inspection and modification of the installation of the charge controller. For the reason described above this AD requires modification of the installation of the charge controller. Required Action(s) and Compliance Time(s): Required as indicated by this AD, unless the action(s) required by this AD have been already accomplished: Modification: (1) For Group 1 powered sailplanes: Within 2 months after the effective date of this AD, modify the installation of the charge controller on the powered sailplane in accordance with the instructions of Action 2 of the TN. Part(s) Installation: (2) For Group 1 and Group 2 powered sailplanes: From the effective date of this AD it is allowed to (re)install the charge controller on a powered sailplane, provided that the installation is compliant with the requirements of paragraph (1) of this AD. Ref. Publications: Schempp-Hirth Technical Note No. 627-16 original issue dated 17 August 2026, and Revision 1 dated 07 October 2026.
