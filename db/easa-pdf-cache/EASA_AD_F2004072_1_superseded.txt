REASONS : Several incomplete inflation of emergency escape slides have been experienced by the manufacturer during tests deployment in their facility. u Investigations revealed that the inflation hose ruptured at its end fitting due to interferrence with slide fabric during inflation. S An incomplete emergency escape slide inflation would delay passengers evacuation. This AD mandates the application of SB A320-25-1338, which introduces an inflation hose retainer. 3. MANDATORY ACTION AND COMPLIANCE TIME: Before June 30, 2007, modify all door emergency escape slides in accordance with SB A320-25-1338. 4.
