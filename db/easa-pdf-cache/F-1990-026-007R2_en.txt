Airworthiness Directive. Translation of ‘Consigne de Navigabilité’ ref. : 90-026-007(B) R2 In case of any difficulty, reference should be made to the French original issue. AIRBUS INDUSTRIE A320 aircraft Interruption of landing gear retraction This

Airworthiness Directive applies to AIRBUS INDUSTRIE A320 aircraft, all models, without AIRBUS INDUSTRIE modification 2190 or without AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-32-1048 embodied. A case of interruption of landing gear retraction has occurred during the simulated failure of an alternator during the retraction sequence. In order to prevent this interruption due to loss of electrical power to the relay 48GA, the following measure is rendered mandatory on the effective date of this

Airworthiness Directive : - Unless already accomplished and before December 1, 1990 modify wiring and electronic component installed by AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-32-1035 in accordance with AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-32-1048. Ref. : AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-32-1035 (modification 21703). AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-32-1048 (modification 21920).
