EASA AD No.: 2026-0192 Reason: Cracking of the affected part was reported during accomplishment of a routine maintenance inspection of the adjacent area. Investigation attributed the crack to a mechanical damage of the anti-corrosion coating, that caused local corrosion and then fatigue crack initiation and propagation. The damage likely occurred during one of the previous maintenance operations. This condition, if not detected and corrected, could lead to structural failure of the affected part, resulting in loss of helicopter control. To address this potential unsafe condition, Leonardo S.p.A. issued the ASB to provide inspection instructions. For the reasons described above, this AD requires a one-time inspection of the affected part and, depending on findings, contacting Leonardo S.p.A. for corrective action instructions. This AD also requires reporting to support the follow-on investigation. This AD is considered an interim action, and further AD action may follow. Required Action(s) and Compliance Time(s): Required as indicated by this AD, unless the action(s) required by this AD have been already accomplished: Inspection(s): (1) For Group 1 helicopters: Within 25 flight hours (FH) after the effective date of this AD, inspect each affected part to detect cracks or corrosions in accordance with the instructions of the ASB. Corrective Action(s): (2) If, during the inspection as required by paragraph (1) of this AD, discrepancies are detected, as specified in the ASB, before next flight, contact Leonardo S.p.A. for approved corrective action instructions and, within the compliance time stated therein, accomplish those instructions accordingly. Using the Inspection Report Form attached to the ASB is an acceptable method to comply with the requirement to contact Leonardo S.p.A. Reporting: (3) If, during the inspection as required by paragraph (1) of this AD, no damage is detected, within 30 days after that inspection report the inspection result to Leonardo S.p.A. Using the Inspection Report Form attached to the ASB is an acceptable method to comply with the requirement to contact Leonardo S.p.A. Part(s) Installation: (4) For Group 1 and Group 2 helicopters: From the effective date of this AD, it is allowed to install any TGB fitting on a helicopter, provided that it is a serviceable part.
