Airworthiness Directive applies to AIRBUS INDUSTRIE A320 aircraft, all models, without AIRBUS INDUSTRIE modification 22561 or without AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-26-1017 (installation of an ambient system or without modification 24548 or without AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-26-1037 In order to void incorrect installation of the lavatory air extraction duct grill, which could reduce the performance of the smoke detection system, the following measures are rendered mandatory on the effective date of this

Airworthiness Directive : - Within 500 flight hours, unless already accomplished, modify on each lavatory, the air extraction duct grill in accordance with the instructions of AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-26-1037. Note : The application of this

Airworthiness Directive cancels the requirements of
