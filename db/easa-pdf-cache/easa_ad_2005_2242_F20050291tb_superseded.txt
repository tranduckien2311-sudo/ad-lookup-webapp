EFFECTIVITY: E AIRBUS A318, A319, A320 and A321 aircraft, all certified models equipped with at least one NORTHROP-GRUMMAN (previously LITTON) ADIRU (AIRBUS modification 24852 or 25108 or 25336 or 26002 or 28218). S 2. REASONS: R Some cases of loss of inertial references have been reported which could reduce the aptitude to control the aircraft due to the non-availability of some parameters (attitude, vertical speed, ground speed, …). When aircraft is fitted with NORTHROP-GRUMMAN (previously LITTON) ADIRU, the clearance between E the shelf and the ADIRU’s is very small, consequently, vibrations during take-off may cause this shelf to hit the top of the ADIRU. Thus excessive G' s in the vertical channel may happen and lead to the loss of parameters. P

Airworthiness Directive (AD) 2002-125, dealing with the same subject, recalled the operational limitations mandated by CN 2001-150 and mandated the modification of the ADIRU shelf supports. U In service events show that the modification of the shelf is not sufficient. The AD at original issue recalled the requirements of AD 2002-125, and extended the operational limitation to all the aircraft equipped with at least one NORTHROP-GRUMMAN (previously LITTON) S ADIRU.
