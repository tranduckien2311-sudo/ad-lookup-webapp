ATA 55 – Stabilizers – Elevators – Inspections / Replacement

Manufacturer(s): Airbus, formerly Airbus Industrie

Applicability: Airbus A319-151N, A319-153N, A319-171N, A319-173N, A321-251N, A321-251NX, A321-252N, A321-252NX, A321-253N, A321-253NX, A321-271N, A321-271NX, A321-272N and A321-272NX aeroplanes, all manufacturer serial numbers (MSN).

Definitions: For the purpose of this AD, the following definitions apply: Affected part: Elevators having a Part Number (P/N) as listed in the Appendix 1 of this AD. Serviceable part: Elevators, eligible for installation in accordance with Airbus approved instructions, that are not an affected part. The SB: Airbus Service Bulletin (SB) A320-55-1071. AD No.: 2026-0182R1 Group 2 aeroplanes are those which are not Group 1 aeroplanes. Affected NEO aeroplanes: Airbus A319-151N, A319-153N, A319-171N, A319-173N, A321-251N, A321-251NX, A321-252N, A321-252NX, A321-253N, A321-253NX, A321-271N, A321-271NX, A321- 272N and A321-272NX aeroplanes.

Reason: It was determined that elevator P/Ns on which Airbus modification (mod) 35515 was not embodied (pre-mod 35515) have been erroneously listed as eligible for installation in the Illustrated Part Catalogue (IPC) for the affected NEO models. However, only post-mod 35515 P/Ns are eligible for installation, according to the type definition of the NEO models. As per EASA AD 2014-0014, these pre-mod 35515 P/N must be inspected to detect, correct, and prevent water ingress. This condition, if not detected and corrected, could lead to disbonding between the affected elevator panel skin and honeycomb core, affecting the structural integrity of the elevator structure, potentially leading to reduced control of the aeroplane. To address this potential unsafe condition, Airbus issued the SB providing instruction for inspection and replacement of the pre-mod 35515 elevators of the NEO fleet. Accordingly, EASA issued AD 2026-0182 requiring to inspect and replace the affected parts. Subsequent to the issuance of that AD, it was determined that the "Affected NEO aeroplanes" definition mistakenly encompassed non-affected aircraft. Furthermore, duplicate part numbers listed in Appendix 1 of this AD have been removed, and a typographical error in the SB definition has been rectified. This AD is revised accordingly.

Required Action(s) and Compliance Time(s): Required as indicated by this AD, unless the action(s) required by this AD have been already accomplished: Inspection: (1) For Group 1 aeroplanes: For each affected part, within 3 months after the effective date of this AD, accomplish a thermographic inspection of that affected part in accordance with the instructions of the SB. (2) For an affected part that, before the effective date of this AD, has been inspected in accordance with the instructions of Airbus SB A320-55-1046 or Airworthiness Limitation Item (ALI) task 552007, the inspection as required by paragraph (1) of this AD can be postponed up to 6 years after the first flight of that affected part after that inspection (see Note 1 of this AD). Note 1: Airbus SB A320-55-1046 or ALI task 552007 are not applicable to the affected NEO aeroplanes. The affected part could have been inspected while installed on a different aeroplane. AD No.: 2026-0182R1 Corrective action(s): (3) If, during the inspection as required by paragraph (1) of this AD, discrepancies, as defined in the SB, are detected, within the compliance time specified in the SB, accomplish the applicable corrective action(s) in accordance with the instructions of the SB. Where the SB requires to contact Airbus for corrective action(s) instructions, this AD requires to contact Airbus before next flight for approved corrective action(s) instructions and, within the compliance time specified therein, to accomplish those instructions accordingly. Modification: (4) For Group 1 aeroplanes: Within 12 months after the effective date of this AD, replace each affected part with a serviceable part in accordance with the instructions of the SB. Part(s) Installation: (5) For Group 1 and Group 2 aeroplanes: From the effective date of this AD, do not install an affected part on any aeroplane (see Note 2 of this AD). Note 2: Removal of an affected part from an aeroplane and subsequent reinstallation of that affected part on the same aeroplane, accomplished during a single maintenance visit, is not considered as ‘install’ as specified in paragraph (5) of this AD .

Ref. Publications: Airbus SB A320-55-1071 original issue dated 31 July 2026. The use of later approved revisions of the above-mentioned document is acceptable for compliance with the requirements of this AD.
