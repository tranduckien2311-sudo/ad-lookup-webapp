D AIRBUS INDUSTRIE A330 aircraft E Inspection of "SAMM" elevator servo-control (ATA 27) D APPLICABILITY: AIRBUS INDUSTRIE A330 aircraft, all certified models and all serial numbers, fitted with "SAMM" elevator servo-control with Part Number SC4800-2 to SC4800-8. E REASONS: A new case of active elevator servo-control piston rod broken was reported by an operator. S The degradation of the self lubricated liner from the eye end spherical bearing is at the origin of this failure. R In case of a rupture of the piston rod on both the active and damping mode servo-controls on the same side, the elevator is no more damped and becomes uncontrollable. Note : For similar reasons, "SAMM" elevator servo-controls fitted with "FRANKENJURA" eye-ends have been subject to

Airworthiness Directive (AD) 98-539-088(B). E Revision 1 of this AD gives more accuracy to the conditions for the accomplishment of the mandatory actions. P COMPLIANCE: The following measures are rendered mandatory and have to be accomplished before accumulation of U 30 months in service since new for each elevator servo-control or within 500 flight hours, following the effective date of this AD, whichever occurs later. 1. Check the play of the piston rod eye-end of the active and damping servo-controls (FIN 2CS1, 2CS2, 3CS1, 3CS2) in accordance with the instructions of the paragraph 3.B.(2) of AIRBUS INDUSTRIE S Service Bulletin (SB) A330-27-3062 R1, unless already previously accomplished in accordance with SB A330-27-3062 at initial issue or Revision 1. 2. Depending on the play measured, for each servo-control, perform the corrective action in accordance with the instructions given in SB A330-27-3062 R1, unless already previously accomplished in accordance with SB A330-27-3062 at initial issue or Revision 1. n/ GH AIRBUS INDUSTRIE March 08, 2000 2000-025-109(B) R1 A330 aircraft
