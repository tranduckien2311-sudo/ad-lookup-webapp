AD No.: 2023-0151 The AMP: The Aircraft Maintenance Programme (AMP) contains the tasks on the basis of which the scheduled maintenance is conducted to ensure the continuing airworthiness of each operated aeroplane. For aeroplanes operated under EU regulation the operator or the owner ensures compliance with the AMP as stipulated in Commission Regulation (EU) 1321/2014. New and/or more restrictive tasks: This includes all tasks that are new and all tasks for which a threshold or interval was reduced, which were introduced through the Variation (as defined in this AD) since the previous ALS Revision that is currently incorporated in the AMP.

Reason: The airworthiness limitations for the Airbus A320 family aeroplanes, which are approved by EASA, are currently defined and published in the Airbus A318/A319/A320/A321 ALS documents. These instructions have been identified as mandatory for continued airworthiness. The tasks and D airworthiness limitations with regard to damage tolerant items are specified in ALS Part 2 DT-ALI. Failure to accomplish these instructions could result in an unsafe condition. E EASA previously issued AD 2022-0085, requiring the actions described in Airbus A318/A319/A320/A321 ALS Part 2 DT ALI Revision 09, and AD 2023-0008, requiring the actions described in Airbus A318/A319/A320/A321 ALS Part 2 DT-ALI Revision 09 Variation 9.2, to introduce D new tasks and limitations, and tasks and limitations for which the threshold or interval was reduced. Since those ADs were issued, Airbus published additionally the Variation, which contains new E and/or more restrictive tasks and limitations. These tasks and limitations are expected to be incorporated into A318/A319/A320/A321 ALS Part 2 DT-ALI at the next revision. S For the reason described above, this AD requires accomplishment of the actions specified in the Variation. EASA AD 2022-0085 and AD 2023-0008 are not superseded by this AD. R

Required Action(s) and Compliance Time(s): Required as indicated, unless accomplished previously: E Maintenance Tasks and Replacement of Life Limited Parts : (1) From the effective date of this AD, within the thresholds and intervals as defined in the Variation (see Note 1 of this AD), accomplish the actions as specified in the Variation, as P applicable to aeroplane model and depending on aeroplane configuration. Where this AD requires a task which is already required per EASA AD 2022-0085 or AD 2023- U 0008, the instructions of the Variation invalidate the instructions of Airbus A318/A319/A320/A321 ALS Part 2 DT-ALI Revision 09 and/or A318/A319/A320/A321 ALS Part 2 DT-ALI Revision 09 Variation 9.2 for that task. S Note 1: For the purpose of this AD, the thresholds and intervals as defined in the ‘Compliance Time’ pages of the ALS include specific compliance times for certain tasks. AD No.: 2023-0151 Corrective Action(s): (2) In case of finding discrepancies during accomplishment of any task as required by paragraph (1) of this AD, before next flight, accomplish the applicable corrective action(s) in accordance with the applicable Airbus maintenance documentation. If a detected discrepancy cannot be corrected by using existing Airbus instructions, before next flight, contact Airbus for approved instructions and accomplish those instructions accordingly. AMP Revision: (3) Within 12 months after the effective date of this AD, revise the approved AMP by incorporating the limitations, tasks and associated threshold and intervals described in the Variation, as applicable to aeroplane model and depending on aeroplane configuration. Recording AD Compliance: D (4) When the AMP of an aeroplane has been revised as required by paragraph (3) of this AD, as applicable, that action ensures continued accomplishment of the tasks as required by paragraphs (1) and (2) of this AD for that aeroplane. Consequently, after revising the AMP, as E required by paragraph (3) of this AD, as applicable, it is not necessary that accomplishment of individual action is recorded for demonstration of AD compliance on a continued basis.
