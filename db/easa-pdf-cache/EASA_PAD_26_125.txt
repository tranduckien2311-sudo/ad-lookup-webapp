ATA 36 – Pneumatic – Pylon / Wing Interface Bleed Duct and Fuel Pipe – Inspection / Modification / Replacement Manufacturer: Airbus Applicability: Airbus A380-841, A380-842 and A380-861 aeroplanes, all manufacturer serial numbers. Definitions: For the purpose of this AD, the following definitions apply: Affected areas: Bleed-air duct and fuel pipe on each of the 4 wing to engine pylon interfaces. Affected parts: The four duct assemblies-bleed air, having Part Number (P/N) L3615006300000 (pylon 1), L3615004800000 (pylon 2), L3615004800100 (pylon 3), or L3615006300100 (pylon 4), as applicable, depending on to which ‘wing to engine pylon # interface’ the part belongs. Serviceable parts: The four d uct assemblies-bleed air, having P/N L3615111700000 (pylon 1), L3615111300000 (pylon 2), L3615111300100 (pylon 3), or L3615111700100 (pylon 4), as applicable, depending on to which ‘wing to engine pylon # interface’ the part belongs. EASA PAD No.: 26-125 The AOT: Airbus Alert Operators Transmission (AOT) A36R003-20. The SB: Airbus Service Bulletin (SB) A380-36-8086. Interim mod: Modification (mod) in accordance with the instructions of Airbus SB A380-28-8074, A380-28-8075, A380-28-8076 and A380-28-8077, or SB A380-28-8078, A380-28-8079, A380-28-8080 and A380-28-8081, as applicable, depending on the installed engine type (Rolls-Royce or Engine Alliance) and related pylon; or Airbus mod 78637, 78638, 78639 and 78640, as applicable; or Airbus Repair Design Approval Forms (RDAF) as listed in each of the (3) columns of Table 1 of this AD, applicable for each of the four (different) engine pylons. Table 1 – Airbus RDAF 80885156/005/2021 80885156/010/2021 80894228/004/2021 80885156/007/2021 80885156/011/2021 80894228/005/2021 80885156/008/2021 80885156/012/2021 80894228/006/2021 80885156/009/2021 80885156/013/2021 80894228/007/2021 The modification SB: Airbus SBs A380-36-8089, A380-36-8090, A380-36-8091 and A380-36-8092, as applicable for the four (different) wing to pylon # interfaces. Groups: Group 1 aeroplanes are those which have not embodied the interim mod. Group 2 aeroplanes are those which have embodied the interim mod. Group 3 aeroplanes are those which have embodied the modification SB. Reason: Occurrences were reported of bleed-air duct detachments due to failure of the bottom welded bracket. This bracket failure could lead to contact between the bleed-air duct and the fuel pipe located below the duct. This condition, if not detected and corrected, could, in case of a fuel leak in combination with a bleed-air leak, create a source of ignition, possibly resulting in an uncontrolled fire. To address this potential unsafe condition, Airbus issued the original issue of the AOT providing inspection instructions. Consequently, EASA issued AD 2020-0162 to require a one-time inspection of the affected areas and, depending on findings, accomplishment of applicable corrective action(s). After that AD was issued, Airbus issued Revision 01 of the AOT, introducing repetitive inspections and an alternative inspection method, and EASA issued AD 2020-0286, retaining the requirements of EASA AD 2020-0162, which was superseded, to require repetitive inspections of the affected areas and, depending on findings, accomplishment of applicable corrective action(s). EASA PAD No.: 26-125 After that AD was issued, Airbus issued the SB, as defined in this AD, introducing the repetitive inspections. Airbus also developed an interim modification and issued the interim mod, as defined in this AD. Consequently, EASA issued AD 2021-0280, later revised, retaining the requirements of EASA AD 2020-0286, which was superseded, requiring in addition embodiment of the interim mod. That AD also defined extended inspection intervals for aeroplanes which have embodied the interim mod, either during production or in service. Since EASA AD 2021-0280R1 was issued, Airbus has redesigned the four (different) duct assemblies- bleed-air, defined as affected parts in this AD, and published the modification SB to provide instructions for replacement of the four affected parts with redesigned parts with improved attachment brackets. For the reason described above, this AD retains the requirements of EASA AD 2021-0280R1, which is superseded, requiring in addition replacement of all (four) affected parts, and introducing this as terminating action for the various repetitive inspections. Required Action(s) and Compliance Time(s): Required as indicated by this AD, unless the action(s) required by this AD have been already accomplished: Repetitive Inspections: (1) For Group 1 aeroplanes: Within 50 flight cycles (FC) or 400 flight hours (FH), whichever occurs first after 04 August 2020 [the effective date of EASA AD 2020-0162], and, thereafter, at intervals not to exceed 50 FC or 400 FH, whichever occurs first, inspect the affected areas in accordance with the instructions of the SB. (2) For Group 2 aeroplanes: Within the compliance time specified in Table 2 of this AD, and, thereafter, at intervals not to exceed 600 FC or 4 400 FH, whichever occurs first, inspect the affected areas in accordance with the instructions of the SB. Table 2 - Threshold Aeroplane Configuration Compliance Time Before exceeding 600 FC or 4 400 FH, whichever Interim mod, as defined in this occurs first since aeroplane’s first flight AD, embodied during production Within 600 FC or 4 400 FH, whichever occurs first, Interim mod embodied in service after embodiment of the interim mod on an aeroplane Corrective Action(s): (3) If, during any inspection as required by paragraph (1) or (2) of this AD, as applicable, any discrepancy is identified as specified in the SB, before next flight, accomplish the applicable corrective action(s) in accordance with the instructions of the SB. EASA PAD No.: 26-125 Interim Modification: (4) For Group 1 aeroplanes: For an aeroplane that, before the effective date of this AD, passed an inspection (no discrepancies found) in accordance with the instructions of the SB, or that, depending on findings, has been corrected, as required by paragraph (3) of this AD: Within 300 FC or 2 400 FH, whichever occurs first after 31 December 2021 [the effective date of EASA AD 2021-0280], modify the bleed-air ducts and fuel pipes on each of the four wing to pylon interfaces in accordance with the instructions of the interim mod. After embodiment of this interim mod, the aeroplane is considered to be a Group 2 aeroplane. Modification: (5) For Group 1 and Group 2 aeroplanes: Within 50 months after the effective date of this AD, modify the aeroplane, by replacing all (four) affected parts with serviceable parts in accordance with the instructions of the modification SB (see Note 1 of this AD). After this modification (replacement of all four affected parts), the aeroplane is considered to be a Group 3 aeroplane. Note 1: Replacement of an affected part with a serviceable part on a certain wing to pylon # interface of the aeroplane can be accomplished during different maintenance visits. This replacement on a certain wing to pylon # interface constitutes terminating action for the repetitive inspections as required by paragraph (1) or (2) of this AD, as applicable, for the affected areas of that wing to pylon # interface. (6) Modification of an aeroplane, by replacing all (four) affected parts with serviceable parts in accordance with the instructions of the modification SB, as required by paragraph (5) of this AD, is an acceptable method to comply with the requirement of paragraph (4) of this AD for that aeroplane. Credit: (7) For Group 1 and Group 2 aeroplanes: Inspections of and corrective actions on an aeroplane, accomplished before 31 December 2021 [the effective date of EASA AD 2021-0280] in accordance with the instructions of the AOT (at any revision), are acceptable to comply with the initial requirements of the paragraphs (1) and (3) of this AD, as applicable, for that aeroplane. Reporting: (8) For Group 1 and Group 2 aeroplanes: Within 30 days after each inspection as required by paragraph (1) or (2) of this AD, as applicable, in case of findings, report the results to Airbus. Terminating Action: (9) Embodiment of the interim mod on an aeroplane as required by paragraph (4) of this AD constitutes terminating action for the repetitive inspections as required by paragraph (1) of this AD for that aeroplane. (10) Embodiment on an aeroplane of the modification SB, as required by paragraph (5) of this AD, constitutes terminating action for the repetitive inspections as required by the paragraph (1) or (2) of this AD, as applicable, for that aeroplane.
