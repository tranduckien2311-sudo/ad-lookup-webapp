D Groups: Group 1 aeroplanes are those which are in post-modification (MOD) and/or post-Service Bulletin (SB) configuration as defined in Table 1 or Table 2 of Appendix 1 of this AD, as applicable. E Group 2 aeroplanes are those which are not Group 1 aeroplanes.

Reason: It has been determined that fatigue cracking may occur in affected areas on aeroplanes having D Sharklets installed during production or in service. This condition, if not detected and corrected, could lead to crack initiation and propagation, E possibly resulting in reduced structural integrity of the wings. To address this potential unsafe condition, Airbus issued the original issue of AOT A57N018-21, to S provide inspection instructions. Consequently, EASA issued AD 2021-0256 to require repetitive detailed visual inspections (DET) of the affected areas, as defined in this AD, and, depending on findings, accomplishment of applicable corrective action(s). R Since that AD was issued, new investigation results highlighted that these inspection must be applied to all models of A319, A320 and A321 aeroplanes in an affected configuration, and the E associated compliance time must be adapted to these configurations. Prompted by this development, Airbus issued the AOT, as defined in this AD. In addition, further investigation results highlighted that the post-repair inspection program(s) in accordance with the SRM tasks for the P affected area is not adequate and that for A321 NEO aeroplanes the bush installation instructions need to be corrected. For the reasons described above, this AD retains the requirements of EASA AD 2021-0256, which is U superseded, expands the Applicability to include NEO aeroplanes and requires accomplishment of the required actions within updated compliance times, as applicable to aeroplane configuration.
