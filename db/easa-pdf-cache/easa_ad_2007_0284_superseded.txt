EASA AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE

AD No.: 2007 - 0284 Date: 12 November 2007 No person may operate an aircraft to which an

Airworthiness Directive applies, except in accordance with the requirements of that

Airworthiness Directive unless otherwise agreed with the Authority of the State of Registry. Type/Model designation(s) : Type Approval Holder’s Name : d AIRBUS A330-200/-300 and A340-300 series e TCDS Number : EASA A.004, EASA A.015 Foreign AD : Not applicable d Supersedure : None e Fuselage – Longitudinal Doubler at Vertical Tail Plane s

ATA 53 (VTP) Attachment Cut out - Inspection / Modification r

Manufacturer(s): AIRBUS (formerly AIRBUS INDUSTRIE) e

Applicability: AIRBUS aircraft A330-200, A330-300 and A340-300 series, all certified models, all serial numbers, on which AIRBUS modification 44205 has p been embodied in production, except those on which AIRBUS modification 52974 or 53223 has been embodied in production. u

Reason: During fatigue tests (EF3) on the A340-600, damages were found in longitudinal doubler at VTP attachment cut out between Frame (FR) 80 and FR86. This damage occurred between 58341 and 72891 simulated Flight Cycles (FC). S Due to the higher Design Service Goal and different design (e.g. doubler thickness) for A330-200/-300 and A340-300 aircraft series, the damage assessment concluded on potential impact on aircraft series part of the above applicability. In order to allow early detection of cracks which could avoid possible crack propagation and consequently to maintain the structural integrity of the upper shell structure between FR80 and FR86, this

Airworthiness Directive (AD) mandates an inspection program of this area using a high frequency eddy current (HFEC) method and a modification to improve the upper shell structure.

Effective Date: 26 November 2007 1/3
