ATA 27 Flight Controls – Elevator Servo-Control Rod Eye-end – Inspection R

Manufacturer(s): AIRBUS (formerly AIRBUS INDUSTRIE) AIRBUS A318-111, A318-112, A318-121, A318-122, A319-111, A319-112, E A319-113, A319-114, A319-115, A319-131, A319-132, A319-133, A320-111,

Applicability: A320-211, A320-212, A320-214, A320-215, A320-216, A320-231, A320-232, A320-233, A321-111, A321-112, A321-131, A321-211, A321-212, A321-213, P A321-231 and A321-232 aircraft, all manufacturer serial numbers. One case of elevator servo-control disconnection has been experienced on an aircraft of the A320 family. Failure occurred at the servo-control rod eye-end. U Further to this finding, additional inspections have revealed cracking at the same location on a number of other servo-control rod eye-ends. In one case, both actuators of the same elevator surface were affected. The root cause of the cracking has not yet been determined and tests are ongoing. It is S anticipated that further actions will be required.

Reason: A dual servo-control disconnection on the same elevator could result in an uncontrolled surface, the elevator surface being neither actuated nor damped, which could lead to reduced control of the aircraft. For the reason described above, this EASA AD requires a one-time inspection of the elevator servo-control rod eye-ends and, in case of findings, the accomplishment of corrective actions.

AD No.: 2008-0149 Required as indicated, unless previously accomplished, although not before accumulation of 10 000 Flight Cycles (FC) since aircraft first flight: (1) For aircraft which have accumulated 10 000 FC or more since first flight at the effective date of this AD: - Within 1 500 FC or 200 days, whichever occurs first after the effective date of this AD, inspect both LH and RH inboard elevator servo-control rod eye-ends in accordance with the instructions of AIRBUS All Operators Telex (AOT) A320-27A1186. - Within 3 000 FC or 400 days, whichever occurs first after the effective date of this AD, inspect both LH and RH outboard elevator servo-control rod eye-ends in accordance with the instructions of AOT A320-27A1186. (2) For aircraft which have accumulated less than 10 000 FC since first flight at the effective date of this AD: Required Action(s) - Within 1 500 FC or 200 days from reaching the threshold of 10 000 FC, and Compliance whichever occurs first, inspect both LH and RH inboard elevator servo- Time(s): D control rod eye-ends in accordance with the instructions of AOT A320- 27A1186. - Within 3 000 FC or 400 days from reaching the threshold of 10 000 FC, whichever occurs first, inspect both LH and RH outboard elevator servo- E control rod eye-ends in accordance with the instructions of AOT A320- 27A1186. (3) In case of findings, before next flight, contact AIRBUS and accomplish the D relevant corrective actions in accordance with the instructions of AOT A320-27A1186. (4) Within 40 days after accomplishment of each inspection as required by this E AD, report the results, including no findings, to AIRBUS. (5) After the effective date of this AD, no person shall install an elevator servo-control rod eye-end on any aircraft, unless it has been inspected in S accordance with the instructions of AOT A320-27A1186.
