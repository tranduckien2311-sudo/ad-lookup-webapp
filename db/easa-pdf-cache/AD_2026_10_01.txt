AGÊNCIA NACIONAL DE AVIAÇÃO CIVIL - BRAZIL BRAZILIAN AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE AD No.: 2026-10-01 Effective Date: 26 Oct. 2026 The following Brazilian Airworthiness Directive (AD), issued by the Agência Nacional de Aviação Civil (ANAC) in accordance with provisions of Chapter IV, Title III of Código Brasileiro de Aeronáutica - Law No. 7,565 dated 19 December 1986 - and Regulamento Brasileiro da Aviação Civil (RBAC) 39, applies to all aircraft registered in the Registro Aeronáutico Brasileiro. No person may operate an aircraft to which this AD applies, unless it has previously complied with the requirements established herein. AD No. 2026-10-01 - EMBRAER / 39-1608. APPLICABILITY: This Airworthiness Directive (AD) applies to Embraer S.A. airplanes model EMB- 505, as identiﬁed in Embraer Service Bulletin N. 505-27-0036, Revision 02, dated August 27, 2026. CANCELLATION / REVISION: Not applicable. REASON: It was identiﬁed the possibility that some newly manufactured airplanes may have been released with rudder trim control system backlash test results beyond the permissible limits due to an error in production line testing. Excessive backlash combined with the loss of one load path in the rudder trim tab control system may result in a Limit Cycle Oscillation (LCO) phenomenon exposing the surrounding structure and systems to unacceptable vibration levels and reducing the airplane controllability. Since this condition may occur in other airplanes of the same type and aﬀects ﬂight safety, a corrective action is required. Thus, suﬃcient reason exists to request compliance with this AD in the indicated time limit. REQUIRED ACTION: Inspection of the airplane rudder trim tab control system and replacement, as applicable, of the attachment parts of the yaw trim actuator, attachment parts and hinge pin of the rudder trim tab surface, yaw trim actuator and its mechanism and rudder trim tab surface, as well.
