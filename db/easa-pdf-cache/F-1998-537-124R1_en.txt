AIRBUS INDUSTRIE A320 aircraft Improvement of the Airborne Ground Check Module (AGCM) low pressure check valve (ATA 29) APPLICABILITY : AIRBUS INDUSTRIE A320 all certified models, all serial numbers without AIRBUS INDUSTRIE modification 27728 or 27189 applied during production or AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-29-1086. REASON : During 2 in-flight events and during one functional test of the Airborne Ground Check Module (AGCM) of the Ram Air Turbine, a blockage of the low pressure check valve was observed. Such a blockage could lead to the loss of the blue hydraulic system when using the Ram Air Turbine (RAT). ACTION : Before december 31, 1999, modify the Airborne Ground Check Module (AGCM) in accordance with the instructions of AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-29-1086. REF. : Service Bulletin AIRBUS INDUSTRIE A320-29-1086 (or any further approved revision). This Revision 1 replaces original AD 98-537-124(B) dated December 30, 1998. EFFECTIVE DATES : Original AD : JANUARY 09, 1999 Revision 1 : OCTOBER 02, 1999 n/ JJD AIRBUS INDUSTRIE September 22, 1999 1998-537-124(B) R1 A320 aircraft
