ATA 79 – Engine Oil – Oil Pump – Operational Prohibition

Manufacturer(s): Rolls-Royce plc

Applicability: RB211 Trent 768-60, 772-60, 772B-60 and Trent 772C-60 engines, all serial numbers (s/n). These engines are known to be installed on, but not limited to, Airbus A330 aeroplanes.

Definitions: For the purpose of this AD, the following definitions apply: The NMSB: Rolls-Royce Alert Non-Modification Service Bulletin (NMSB) RB211−79−AL442 Revision 1. The NMSB has an ‘A’ (Alert) in the number, but an earlier or later revision may not have that ‘A’. This kind of change does not effectively alter the publication references. Serviceable part: Oil pump having Part Number (P/N) LR37940D, LR37940DA, LR37940DB, LR37940DC, LR37940DD, LR37940F, LR37940FA, LR37940FB, LR37940FC, LR37940FD, LR37940FE, LR37940FF, LR37940FG, LR37940J, LR37940K, LR37940L, LR37940N, LR37940P, LR47390B and P/N LR47390D identified as “not affected parts” in section 1.D of the NMSB, as defined in this AD. AD No.: 2026-0189 Affected part: Oil pump having P/N LR37940D, LR37940DA, LR37940DB, LR37940DC, LR37940DD, LR37940F, LR37940FA, LR37940FB, LR37940FC, LR37940FD, LR37940FE, LR37940FF, LR37940FG, LR37940J, LR37940K, LR37940L, LR37940N, LR37940P, LR47390B and P/N LR47390D which are not serviceable parts, as defined in this AD. Affected engine: An engine that has an affected part installed.

Reason: Occurrences of non-conformances were reported, leading to low-oil-pressure events on low life oil pumps. Subsequent investigation identified a potential failure mode affecting the oil pump input-coupling shear neck. This condition, if not corrected, could lead to a dual in-flight shutdown on aeroplanes equipped with two affected engines, resulting in reduced control of the aeroplane. To address this potential unsafe condition, Rolls-Royce published the original issue of the NMSB RB211−79−AL440 providing instructions for de-pairing of engines on twin-engine aeroplanes, ensuring that no affected parts are installed on both engines. Consequently, EASA issued Emergency AD 2026-0172-E to require de-pairing of the engines and to ensure that only one affected part is installed on twin-engine aeroplanes. Since that AD was issued, Rolls-Royce published the NMSB, as defined in this AD, expanding the criteria for identifying not affected oil pumps. The life threshold is increased from 135 flight hours (FH) to 400 FH and requires rectification at an approved maintenance facility for all affected units. Furthermore, the population of oil pump P/Ns originally listed in the Emergency AD 2026-0172-E (P/N LR47390D, LR37940P, LR37940DD, LR47390B, and P/N LR37940N) has been extended to include the following additional P/N - P/N LR37940D, LR37940DA, LR37940DB, LR37940DC, LR37940F, LR37940FA, LR37940FB, LR37940FC, LR37940FD, LR37940FE, LR37940FF, LR37940FG, LR37940J, LR37940K and P/N LR37940L. For the reasons described above, this AD partially retains the requirements of EASA AD 2026-0172-E, which is superseded. This AD also prohibits twin-engine operation with affected parts installed on both engines and prohibits installation of affected part on an engine. Further, this AD only allows installation of serviceable parts, as defined in this AD.

Required Action(s) and Compliance Time(s): Required as indicated by this AD, unless the action(s) required by this AD have been already accomplished: Operational Prohibition: (1) From 7 days after the effective date of this AD, concurrent operation of an affected part on both engines of a twin-engine aeroplane is prohibited. I f an affected part is installed on one engine, the opposite engine must be equipped with a serviceable part. (2) From the effective date of this AD, it is allowed to install an oil pump on an engine provided the part is a serviceable part, as defined in this AD. AD No.: 2026-0189 (3) From the effective date of this AD, it is allowed to install an engine on an aeroplane, provided the engine is equipped with an oil pump that is a serviceable part, as defined in this AD. Acceptable Method(s): (4) Replacement of an affected part with a serviceable part on an engine in accordance with approved Rolls-Royce maintenance instructions is an acceptable method to comply with the requirement of paragraph (1) of this AD. (5) Replacement of one of the affected engines with an engine equipped with a serviceable part in accordance with approved Rolls-Royce maintenance instructions is an acceptable method to comply with the requirement of paragraph (1) of this AD.

Ref. Publications: Rolls-Royce Alert NMSB RB211−79−AL442 Revision 1 dated 21 September 2026. The use of later approved revisions of the above-mentioned document is acceptable for compliance with the requirements of this AD.
