Manufacturer(s): Airbus (formerly Airbus Industrie) R

Applicability: Airbus A330 aeroplanes, models -201, -202, -203, -223, -243, -301, -302, - 303, -321, -322, -323, -341, -342 and -343, all manufacturer serial numbers except those on which Airbus modification 51790 has been embodied in production or Airbus Service Bulletin (SB) A330-31-3066 or E SB A330-31-3082 or SB A330-31-3093 or SB A330-31-3105 has been embodied in Service. Airbus A340 aeroplanes, models -211, -212, -213, -311, -312, -313, all P manufacturer serial numbers.

Reason: Further to the accident which occurred to a Boeing 747-131 (flight TWA800), the FAA published SFAR 88 (Special Federal Aviation Regulation 88). U By mail referenced 04/00/02/07/01-L296 of March 4th, 2002 and 04/00/02/07/03-L024 of February 3rd, 2003 the JAA recommended to the National Aviation Authorities (NAA) the application of a similar regulation. S The aim of this regulation is to require all holders of type certificates for transport aircraft certified after 01 January 1958 with a capacity of 30 passengers or more, or a payload of 3 402 kg or more, to carry out a definition review against explosion hazards. AD No.: 2010-0089 To be compliant with SFAR88/JAA INT/POL 25/12 requirements, this AD requires the installation of the updated FWC software standard which ensures correct operation of the APU bleed leak detection system before each flight.

Effective Date: 24 May 2010 Required action(s) Required as indicated, unless already accomplished: and Compliance (1) No later than 6 months after the effective date of this AD install on Time(s): both FWC: - the FWC software standard T3 (part number (P/N) LA2E20202T30000) in accordance with the instructions defined in Airbus SB A330-31-3146, or D - the FWC software standard L11 (P/N LA2E0060D110000) in accordance with the instructions defined in Airbus SB A340-31-4125, as applicable to the aeroplane model. E (2) Compliance with the requirements of paragraph (1) of this AD requires the prior or concurrent accomplishment of the instructions contained in the concurrent requirements section of SB A330-31-3146 or SB D A340-31-4125, as applicable to the aeroplane model.

Ref. Publications: Airbus Service Bulletin A330-31-3146 at Original issue, Airbus Service Bulletin A340-31-4125 at Original issue. E The use of later approved revisions of these documents is acceptable for compliance with the requirements of this AD.
