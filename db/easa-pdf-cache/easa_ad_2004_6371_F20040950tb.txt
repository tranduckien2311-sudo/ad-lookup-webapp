EFFECTIVITY: This

Airworthiness Directive (AD) applies to CFM INTERNATIONAL, CFM56-2C, CFM56-3 series and CFM56-5 series turbofan engines fitted with fuel pumps with solid bronze bearings. These engines are installed on, but not limited to, AIRBUS A319 and A320, BOEING 737 and MCDONNELL DOUGLAS DC-8. Spare engines and spare pumps ar also affected. 2. REASON: Several failures of the main fuel pump solid bronze bearings caused fuel filter, then fuel manifold clogging leading, in some instances, to low pressure turbine case burn through. 3. MANDATORY ACTION AND COMPLIANCE TIME: st Either at next engine shop visit, or at next main fuel pump replacement, or before January 1 , 2007, whichever occurs first, replace main fuel pumps equipped with solid bronze bearings with pumps equipped with bi-metal aluminium/bronze bearings in according with the Service Bulletins listed paragraph 4 below. After the effective date of this AD, it is forbidden to install a main fuel pump with bronze bearings on the concerned engines. Definition: An engine shop visit is defined as any maintenance that includes the separation of an engine casing flange. n/BB
