AD No.: 2026-0172-E Affected part: Oil pump having P/N LR47390D, LR37940P, LR37940DD, LR47390B or P/N LR37940N which are not serviceable parts. Affected engine: An engine that has an affected part installed.

Reason: Occurrences of non-conformances were reported, leading to low-oil-pressure events on low life oil pumps. Subsequent investigation identified a potential failure mode affecting the oil pump input-coupling shear neck. This condition, if not corrected, could lead to a dual in-flight shutdown on aeroplanes equipped with two affected engines, resulting in reduced control of the aeroplane. To address this potential unsafe condition, Rolls-Royce issued the NMSB providing instructions for de-pairing of engines on twin-engine aeroplanes, ensuring that no affected parts are installed on D both engines. E For the reasons described above, this AD requires de-pairing of the engines and ensures that only one affected part is installed on twin-engine aeroplanes. This AD is considered to be an interim action, and further AD action may follow. D

Required Action(s) and Compliance Time(s): E Required as indicated by this AD, unless the action(s) required by this AD have been already accomplished: S De-pairing of engines: (1) From the effective date of this AD do not operate an aeroplane having two affected engines installed. R Additional Requirements(s): E (2) Replacement of an affected part with a serviceable part on an engine is an acceptable method to comply with the de-pairing instructions of the paragraph (1) of this AD. P (3) From the effective date of this AD, it is allowed to reinstall an affected part removed from an affected engine as specified in paragraph (2) of this AD, provided it has been rectified in section 3.B of the NMSB before reinstallation. U (4) Replacement of one of the affected engines with a non-affected engine is an acceptable method to comply with the de-pairing instructions of requirement of paragraph (1) of this AD. S (5) From the effective date of this AD, it is allowed to reinstall an engine removed as specified in paragraph (4) of this AD, provided that the affected part has been replaced with a serviceable part in accordance with approved maintenance instructions.
