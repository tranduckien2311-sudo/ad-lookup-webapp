AIRBUS INDUSTRIE A319/A320/A321 aircraft Rockwell Collins Radio altimeter software anomalies (ATA 34) APPLICABILITY: AIRBUS INDUSTRIE all certified A319, A320 and A321 models, all serial numbers, equipped with ROCKWELL COLLINS radio altimeter LRA 700 P/N 622-4542-020 installed by AIRBUS INDUSTRIE modification 20259 or AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-34-1030 which have not received AIRBUS INDUSTRIE modification 26017 or AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-31-1106 (installation of FWC E2 P/N 350E017271616). REASONS: Laboratory tests have revealed software anomalies of the ROCKWELL COLLINS LRA 700. Such anomalies could lead to erroneous information with no alarm detection on aircraft not equipped with a Flight Warning Computer (FWC) at standard E2 or above. ACTIONS: Within 10 days, insert and follow in Aircraft Flight Manual the Temporary Revision (T.R.) No 2.05.00/43 DGAC approved September 16, 1999. REF. : AFM TR 2.05.00/43 approved September 16, 1999 AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-34-1030 (or any further approved revision) AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-31-1106 (or any further approved revision) EFFECTIVE DATE : JANUARY 22, 2000 n/ JJD AIRBUS INDUSTRIE January 12, 2000 2000-004-142(B) A319/A320/A321 aircraft
