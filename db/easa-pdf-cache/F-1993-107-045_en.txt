Airworthiness Directive applies to AIRBUS INDUSTRIE A320 aircraft MSN's 006, 008, 011, 017, 018, 039, 042 and 045 to 047 included. Airworthiness Directive : - Before accumulation of 14,000 flights, reinforce left frame 20 between stringers 30 and 32, in accordance with instructions given in AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-53-1017. No further action is required after accomplishment of either this
