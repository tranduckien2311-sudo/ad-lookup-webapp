AIRBUS A330 aircraft FMGEC Computer - “Incorrect Nav Guidance upon F-PLN sequencing” & "No Waypoint Sequencing" (ATA 22) APPLICABILITY: AIRBUS A330 aircraft, models -202, -223, -243, -301, -321, -322, -323, -341, -342, -343, all serial numbers except the following aircraft: - Aircraft which have received on production AIRBUS modification 49104 (or AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin (SB) A330-22-3035) for A330 GE, (FMGEC standard L10), - Aircraft which have received on production AIRBUS modification 49105 (or SB A330-22-3036) for A330 RR/PW, (FMGEC standard L10), - Aircraft which have received on production AIRBUS modification 47457 (or SB A330-22-3034) for A330 GE, (FMGEC standard P1), - Aircraft which have received on production AIRBUS modification 47462 (or SB A330-22-3026) for A330 RR/PW, (FMGEC standard P1). REASONS: Some A330 operators equipped with FMGEC standard L9 reported several anomalies of no waypoint sequencing in “NAV” mode. In addition, it was reported by some operators equipped with FMGEC standard L6 and L7 an anomaly of the FMGEC software. These two anomalies, so called “Incorrect Nav Guidance upon F-PLN sequencing” and “no waypoint sequencing” have led the aircraft not to follow the exact trajectory inserted in the flight plan.

Airworthiness Directive (AD) 2000-446-128(B) (“No waypoint sequencing”) and 2000-445-127(B) (“Incorrect Nav Guidance upon F-PLN sequencing”) were issued to mandate respectively the Flight Manual Temporary Revisions (FM TR) No. 4.03.00/17 and 4.03.00/16, both DGAC approved on September 13, 2000, and their related procedures. This AD aims at restating the enforcement of the above FM TR' s and at mandating the replacement of all the FMGEC standard L6, L7 and L9 by FMGEC standard L10 or P1.
