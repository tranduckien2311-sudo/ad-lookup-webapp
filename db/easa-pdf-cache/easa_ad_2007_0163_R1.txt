ATA 27 Flight Controls – Elevator Backlash – Inspection

Manufacturer(s): Airbus (formerly Airbus Industrie)

Applicability: Airbus A318-111, A318-112, A318-121, A318-122, A319-111, A319-112, A319-113, A319-114, A319-115, A319-131, A319-132, A319-133, A320-211, A320-212, A320-214, A320-215, A320-216, A320-231, A320-232, A320-233, A321-111, A321-112, A321-131, A321-211, A321-212, A321-213, A321-231 and A321-232 aeroplanes, all manufacturer serial numbers (MSN).

Reason: Some operators have reported airframe vibration under specific flight conditions including gusts. Investigations have revealed that under such conditions, vibrations may occur when the hinge moment of the elevator is close to zero, associated to elevator free-play. DGAC France issued AD 2002-514 to address and correct this problem, requiring repetitive inspection/measuring of the elevator backlash and, depending on findings, accomplishment of applicable corrective actions. EASA AD 2007-0163 retained the requirements of DGAC AD 2002-514 R1, which was superseded, expanding the Applicability to include A318 aeroplanes and to extend the inspection interval from 18 to 20 months. Since that AD was issued, the results of further analysis have shown that the compliance times can safely be extended. For the reason described above, this AD is revised to extend the inspection threshold and interval from 20 to 24 months. TE.CAP.00110-003 © European Aviation Safety Agency. Effective Date: Revision 1 : 02 January 2014 Original issue : 25 June 2007 Required Action(s) Required as indicated, unless accomplished previously: and Compliance (1) For all aeroplanes: Time(s): (1.1) Within 24 months from aeroplane entry to service, or within 24 months since the last inspection in accordance with AIRBUS A318/A319/A320/A321 AMM task 27-34-00-200-001, whichever occurs later, inspect/measure the elevator backlash and apply all necessary corrective actions in accordance with the instructions of AIRBUS A318/A319/A320/A321 AMM task 27-34-00-200-001, issued on February 1997, or later revision. (1.2) Thereafter, at intervals not exceeding 24 months, repeat the inspection/measurement of the elevator backlash and apply all necessary corrective actions in accordance with the instructions of AIRBUS A318/A319/A320/A321 AMM task 27-34-00-200-001. (2) For all aeroplanes that have not received modification 26094 in production: DGAC France AD 2002-514 R1 required the setting of elevator surface to 0.5° up in accordance with the instructions of Airbus Service Bulletin (SB) A320-27-1114 at any approved revision dated before 31 March 2003. For aeroplanes where this has not already been accomplished, before next flight after the effective date of this AD, modify the aeroplane in accordance with the instructions of Airbus SB A320- 27-1114 Revision 05, or a later approved revision. (3) For A320 aeroplanes with MSN below 1131, and having received modification 26094 in production: DGAC France AD 2002-514 R1 required the verification of the position of the tail cone triangle in accordance with the instructions given by SB A320- 27-1132 within 800 flight hours (FH) after 12 October 2002, the effective date of that AD. For aeroplanes where this has not already been accomplished, before the accumulation of 800 FH after 12 October 2002, or before next flight, whichever occurs later after the effective date of this AD, verify the position of the tail cone triangle in accordance with the instructions of Airbus SB A320-27-1132 Revision 01, or a later approved revision. (4) Accomplishment of the actions as required by paragraphs (2) and (3) of this AD does not constitute terminating action for the repetitive inspections and measurements required by paragraph (1) of this AD.

Ref. Publications: Airbus AMM task 27.34.00.200.001 issued on February 1997. Airbus SB A320-27-1132 Revision 01 dated 19 June 2002. Airbus SB A320-27-1114 Revision 05 dated 14 March 2001. The use of later approved revisions of these documents is acceptable for compliance with the requirements of this AD.
