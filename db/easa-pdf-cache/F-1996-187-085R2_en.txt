Airworthiness Directive applies to AIRBUS INDUSTRIE A320 aircraft, all certified models and all serial numbers without AIRBUS INDUSTRIE modifications 21574 (PM 2025) or 21999 or AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-32-1139 R1 or further approved revisions. In order to avoid the interruption of the landing gear retraction cycle in case of electrical power loss due to the rupture of the capacitor of the retraction control circuit, the following measure is rendered mandatory on the effective date of this

Airworthiness Directive : - Before September 21, 1997 in accordance with AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-32-1139 Rev. 1, replace capacitor 57GA installed in electronic rack 90VU by an electrolyte capacitor with tantalum casing. Ref. : AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-32-1139 Rev. 1 (or further approved revisions) This Revision 2 replaces the Revision 1 dated October 23, 1996.
