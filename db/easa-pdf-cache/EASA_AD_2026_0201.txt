EASA AD No.: 2026-0201 Airworthiness Directive AD No.: 2026-0201 Issued: 26 October 2026 Note: This Airworthiness Directive (AD) is issued by EASA, acting in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2018/1139 on behalf of the European Union, its Member States and of the European third countries that participate in the activities of EASA under Article 129 of that Regulation. This AD is issued in accordance with Regulation (EU) 748/2012, Part 21.A.3B. In accordance with Regulation (EU) 1321/2014 Annex I Part M.A.301, or Annex Vb Part ML.A.301, as applicable, the continuing airworthiness of an aircraft shall be ensured by accomplishing any applicable ADs. Consequently, no person may operate an aircraft to which an AD applies, except in accordance with the requirements of that AD, unless otherwise specified by the Agency [Regulation (EU) 1321/2014 Annex I Part M.A.303, or Annex Vb Part ML.A.303, as applicable] or agreed with the Authority of the State of Registry [Regulation (EU) 2018/1139, Article 71 exemption]. Design Approval Holder’s Name: Type/Model designation(s): AIRBUS HELICOPTERS DEUTSCHLAND GmbH MBB-BK117 helicopters Effective Date: 09 November 2026 TCDS Number(s): EASA.R.010 Foreign AD: Not applicable Supersedure: None ATA 32 – Landing Gear – Emergency Flotation System Adhesive Label – Removal Manufacturer(s): Airbus Helicopters (AH); Airbus Helicopters Deutschland GmbH; Kawasaki Heavy Industries Ltd.; and Airbus Helicopters Inc. Applicability: MBB-BK117 D-2 (all variants) and D-3 helicopters, serial numbers 20001 to 20360 inclusive. Definitions: For the purpose of this AD, the following definitions apply: The ASB: AH Alert Service Bulletin (ASB) MBB-BK117-25-67-0006. Affected label: Emergency Flotation System (EFS) adhesive label Part Number 214794-0. Groups: Group 1 helicopters are those that have an affected label installed. Groups 2 helicopters are those that do not have an affected label installed. Reason: An investigation has revealed that a non-certified EFS adhesive label, displaying incorrect EFS gas cylinder pressure limits, was listed in the MBB-BK117 D-2 Illustrated Parts Catalogue and therefore may have been installed on the structure of the helicopter. EASA AD No.: 2026-0201 This condition, if not corrected, could lead to an incomplete inflation of the floats during ditching, potentially preventing timely evacuation from the helicopter and consequent injury to occupants. To address this potential unsafe condition, AH issued the ASB, to provide instructions for removal of the affected label. For the reason described above, this AD requires removal of those affected labels. Required Action(s) and Compliance Time(s): Required as indicated by this AD, unless the action(s) required by this AD have been already accomplished: Removal: (1) For Group 1 helicopters: Within 18 months after the effective date of this AD, remove the affected label in accordance with the instructions of the ASB. Part(s) Installation: (2) For Group 1 and Group 2 helicopters: From the effective date of this AD, do not install an affected label on any helicopter. Ref. Publications: AH ASB MBB-BK117-25-67-0006 original issue (Issue 001) dated 08 September 2026. The use of later approved revisions of the above-mentioned document is acceptable for compliance with the requirements of this AD. Remarks: 1. Such components may be installed under an FAA Parts Manufacturer Approval (PMA), Supplemental Type Certificate (STC) or other modification.
