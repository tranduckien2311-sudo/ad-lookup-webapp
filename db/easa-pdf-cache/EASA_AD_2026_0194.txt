AD No.: 2026-0194 The additional abnormal procedures: The following abnormal procedures, as identified in the AFM-S R16: • ABN-21 AIR COND / PRESS / VENT - COND - MD VENT FAULT • ABN-21 AIR COND / PRESS / VENT - COND - MIXER OVERPRESS • ABN-21 AIR COND / PRESS / VENT - COND - MD ISOL FAULT

Reason: It was identified that the EFW AFM-S for A330 P2F Rev. 15 does not include abnormal procedures related to the following amber caution messages: • COND MD VENT FAULT • COND MD ISOL FAULT • COND MIXER OVERPRESS Furthermore, discrepancies have been reported in the related operating procedures provided within the EFW FCOM-S for A330 P2F Rev. 9 and in the EFW QRH-S for A330 P2F Rev. 6. This condition, if not corrected, could lead to increased workload of the flight crew and application of incorrect procedures. To address this potential unsafe condition, EFW issued Flight Operations Transmission EFW-E-FOT-00026 to inform aircrews of the discrepancies and define interim procedures, and, thereafter, the AFM-S R16, which includes the additional abnormal procedures, the FCOM-S R10 and the QRH-S R7, which include amended operating procedures. For the reasons described above, this AD requires implementation of the additional abnormal procedures.

Required Action(s) and Compliance Time(s): Required as indicated by this AD, unless the action(s) required by this AD have been already accomplished: AFM Amendment: (1) Within 30 days after the effective date of this AD, implement the additional abnormal procedures, inform all flight crews and, thereafter, operate the aeroplane accordingly. (2) Amending the applicable AFM-S of an aeroplane by incorporating the AFM-S R16 is an acceptable method to comply with the requirements of paragraph (1) of this AD for that aeroplane.

Ref. Publications: EFW A330 P2F AFM-S Rev. 16 dated 15 January 2026. The use of later approved revisions of the above-mentioned document is acceptable for compliance with the requirements of this AD.
