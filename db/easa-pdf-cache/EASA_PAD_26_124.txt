EASA PAD No.: 26-124 Note 1: A review of aeroplane maintenance records is an acceptable method to determine whether a repair has been accomplished on a cargo door seal, provided those records can be relied upon for that purpose. As an alternative to the review of aeroplane maintenance records, the SB provides instructions for a visual inspection of the cargo door seal. Serviceable part: Any FWD and aft cargo door seal, eligible for installation in accordance with Airbus approved instructions, that is not an affected part. Groups: Group 1 aeroplanes are those that have an affected part installed. Group 2 aeroplanes are those that do not have any affected parts installed. Reason: Airbus identified that maintenance procedure MP A350‑A‑52‑33‑XX‑00ZZZ‑685Z‑A has been incorrectly published as a permanent repair, even if it was designed and validated as a temporary repair. Application of this repair may lead to unpredictable cargo door seal behaviour, resulting in potentially degraded airtightness and reduced halon retention capability in the cargo compartment. This condition, if not corrected, could affect the fire extinguishing system efficiency in the cargo compartments, possibly resulting in failure of the system to contain a cargo compartment fire. To address this potential unsafe condition, Airbus issued the SB to provide replacement instructions of the affected parts. For the reasons described above, this AD requires replacement of the affected parts with serviceable parts. Required Action(s) and Compliance Time(s): Required as indicated by this AD, unless the action(s) required by this AD have been already accomplished: Part(s) Replacement: (1) For Group 1 aeroplanes: Within 12 months after the effective date of this AD, replace each affected part with a serviceable part in accordance with the instructions of the SB. Additional Maintenance Requirements: (2) For Group 1 and Group 2 aeroplanes: From the effective date of this AD, do not accomplish any maintenance action on any aeroplane in accordance with the instructions of the MP task, as defined in this AD. Part(s) Installation: (3) For Group 1 and Group 2 aeroplanes: From the effective date of this AD, do not (re)install on any aeroplane any affected part, and do not install on any aeroplane a cargo door having an affected part installed.
