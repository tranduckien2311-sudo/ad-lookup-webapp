AD No.: 2019-0146 These engines are known to be installed on, but not limited to, Airbus A318, A319, A320, A321 and A340 aeroplanes, and Boeing 737-600, 737-700, 737-800 and 737-900 aeroplanes.

Definitions: For the purpose of this AD, the following definitions apply: The applicable S/B: CFM International CFM56-5B Service Bulletin (S/B) 72-1074, CFM56-5C S/B 72- 0794, or CFM56-7B S/B 72-1042, as applicable. Affected part: A rotating air high pressure turbine (HPT) front seal, having part number (P/N) 1795M36P01 or P/N 1795M36P02, and having s/n GWNDN949 to GWNSE969 inclusive, or having s/n GWN000CE to GWN0990L inclusive; excluding those for which it can be shown it has never been D removed from its original HPT disk and re-installed onto a different HPT disk; and excluding those having s/n GWN08ND7, GWN0923A, GWN0971E, GWN098A1, GWN098W6, GWN098W8, GWN098WA or GWN0990G. E Serviceable part: Any rotating air HPT front seal that is not an affected part. Groups: Group 1 engines are those that have an affected part installed. Group 2 engines are those D that do not have an affected part installed. Re-installation: For an affected part, first in-service re-installation on an HPT disk other than the HPT disk on which that affected part was originally installed in production. E

Reason: Occurrences have been reported of finding cracked rotating air HPT front seals during in-shop S inspections. Subsequent investigation determined that certain rotating air HPT front seals, if removed from an HPT disk and re-installed on a different HPT disk, may be susceptible to similar cracks. R This condition, if not detected and corrected, could lead to engine in-flight shut-down, possibly resulting in reduced control of the aeroplane. E To address this potential unsafe condition, CFM International issued the applicable S/B, providing instructions to replace the affected parts. P For the reason stated above, this AD requires replacing the affected parts, and prohibits re-installation thereof.

Required Action(s) and Compliance Time(s): U Required as indicated, unless accomplished previously: Part Replacement: S (1) For Group 1 engines: Within the compliance time as defined in Table 1 of this AD, as applicable, replace the affected part with a serviceable part in accordance with the instructions of the applicable S/B.
