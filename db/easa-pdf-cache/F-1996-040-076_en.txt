Airworthiness Directive applies to AIRBUS INDUSTRIE A320 aircraft, all models, without AIRBUS INDUSTRIE modification 22119 or modification 21999 or without AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-24-1054. In order to improve avionic equipment protection against water contamination in the case of accidental spillage in the cabin or galley area, the following measures are rendered mandatory on the effective date of this

Airworthiness Directive : - Within 3 years, install drip pans and additional drain gutters on racks 90VU and 91VU in accordance with the instructions given in AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-24-1054. No further action is required after accomplishment of either this

Airworthiness Directive or AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-24-1054. Ref. : AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-24-1054 (or any further approved revision) EFFECTIVE DATE : FEBRUARY 24, 1996 v/JYR AIRBUS INDUSTRIE February 14, 1996 96-040-076(B) A320 Aircraft
