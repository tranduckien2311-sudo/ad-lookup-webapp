AIRBUS INDUSTRIE A330 aircraft Reinforcement of fuselage between FR 31 and FR 37.1 (ATA 53) APPLICABILITY: AIRBUS INDUSTRIE A330 aircraft, models -301, -321, -322, -323, -341, -342 and -343, except aircraft which have received AIRBUS INDUSTRIE modification 46636 in production or modified in service in accordance with AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin (SB) A330-53-3090 at original issue or any further approved revision. REASONS: It was evident during fuselage fatigue tests cracks, initiation and propagation in the longitudinal buttstrap at stringer 9 between frame 35 and 37 and at frame 31 (stringer 9 to 15) and at frame 37.1 (stringer 5 to 15). This situation, if not corrected, could lead to reduced fuselage structural integrity. COMPLIANCE: The following measures are rendered mandatory on the effective date of this

Airworthiness Directive (AD): 1. Unless already accomplished, before accumulation of 15,000 flights since first flight, perform, after removal of the fasteners, a roto-test inspection of the fasteners holes of the affected fuselage joints, in accordance with the instructions given in the SB A330-53-3090 Revision 2. 2. If no crack is found, reinforce the fuselage structure between frame 31 and 37.1 in accordance with the requirement of the SB A330-53-3090 Revision 2. 3. If necessary, perform an additional HFEC inspection if the inspection required by the paragraph 1 of this AD has revealed cracks, then contact AIRBUS INDUSTRIE before next flight in order to define a suitable repair solution. REF.: AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A330-53-3090 Revision 2 (any further approved revision of this SB is acceptable). EFFECTIVE DATE : MARCH 17, 2001 n/ GH AIRBUS INDUSTRIE March 07, 2001 2001-075(B) A330 aircraft
