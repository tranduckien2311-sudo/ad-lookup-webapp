New and/or more restrictive tasks: This includes all tasks that are new and all tasks for which a threshold or interval was reduced, which were introduced into the ALS (as defined in this AD) since the previous ALS Revision that is currently incorporated in the AMP.

Reason: The airworthiness limitations applicable to fuel airworthiness limitation items for the Airbus A320 family aeroplanes, which are approved by EASA, are currently defined and published in the Airbus A318/A319/A320/A321 ALS Part 5 document. These instructions have been identified as mandatory for continued airworthiness. D Failure to accomplish these instructions could result in an unsafe condition. EASA previously issued AD 2018-0231 to require the actions as specified in Airbus E A318/A319/A320/A321 ALS Part 5 Revision 05. Since that AD was issued, Airbus published the ALS, as defined in this AD, to introduce new and/or more restrictive tasks and limitations, and new A320 family models were certified and added to the D Applicability. For the reason described above, this AD supersedes EASA AD 2018-0231, expands the Applicability E and requires accomplishment of the actions specified in the ALS.

Required Action(s) and Compliance Time(s): S Required as indicated by this AD, unless the actions required by this AD have been already accomplished: R Maintenance Tasks and Replacement of Life Limited Parts : (1) From the effective date of this AD, accomplish the following actions, as specified in the ALS (see Note 1 of this AD), as applicable to aeroplane model and depending on aeroplane E configuration: (1.1) Replace each component before exceeding the applicable life limit, and P (1.2) Within the thresholds and intervals (see Note 1 of this AD), accomplish all applicable maintenance tasks. U Note 1: For the purpose of this AD, the thresholds and intervals, as defined in the ‘Compliance Time’ pages of the ALS, include specific compliance times for certain tasks. S Corrective Action(s): (2) In case of finding discrepancies during accomplishment of any task as required by paragraph (1) of this AD, before next flight, accomplish the applicable corrective action(s) in accordance with the applicable Airbus maintenance documentation.
