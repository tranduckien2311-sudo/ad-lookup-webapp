ATA 05 – Time Limits / Maintenance Checks – Damage Tolerant Airworthiness Limitation Items – ALS Part 2 – Amendment s

Manufacturer(s): r Airbus, formerly Airbus Industrie

Applicability: Airbus A330-201, A330-202, A330-203, A330-223, A330-223F, A330-243, A330-243F, A330-301, A330-302, A330-303, A330-321, A330-322, A330-323, A330-341, A330-342, A330-343, A330-841 e and A330-941 aeroplanes, all manufacturer serial numbers.

Definitions: For the purpose of this AD, the following definitions apply: p The ALS: Airbus A330 Airworthiness Limitations Section (ALS) Part 2 Revision 07 Issue 02. The AMP: The approved Aircraft Maintenance Programme (AMP) on the basis of which the operator or the owner ensures the continuing airworthiness of each operated aeroplane. For A330 u aeroplanes operated under EU regulation, compliance with the approved AMP is required by Commission Regulation (EU) 1321/2014, Part M.A.301, paragraph (c). AD No.: 2025-0015 New and/or more restrictive tasks: This includes all tasks that are new and all tasks for which a threshold or interval was reduced, which were introduced into the ALS (as defined in this AD) since the previous ALS revision that is currently incorporated in the AMP.

Reason: The Damage Tolerant Airworthiness Limitation Items for the Airbus A330 aeroplanes, which are approved by EASA, are currently defined and published in the Airbus A330 ALS Part 2 document. These instructions have been identified as mandatory for continued airworthiness. d Failure to accomplish these instructions could result in an unsafe condition. EASA previously issued AD 2024-0011 to require the actions as specified in Airbus A330 ALS Part 2 at Revision 06 Issue 02. e Since that AD was issued, Airbus published the ALS, as defined in this AD, to introduce new and/or more restrictive tasks. For the reason described above, this AD retains the requirements of EASA AD 2024-0011, which is d superseded, and requires accomplishment of the actions specified in the ALS. After the publication of the Proposal to issue this AD, referencing the original issue of ALS Part 2 Revision 07, Airbus published ALS Part 2 Revision 07 Issue 02, to correct typographical errors in the e applicability of certain tasks. The ALS, as defined in this AD, now refer to this Issue 02. Additional consultation has not been considered in the interest of operators.

Required Action(s) and Compliance Time(s): s Required as indicated by this AD, unless the action(s) required by this AD have been already accomplished: r Maintenance Tasks: (1) From the effective date of this AD, within the thresholds and intervals (see Note 1 of this AD), accomplish all the applicable maintenance tasks, as specified in the ALS, as applicable to aeroplane model and depending on aeroplane configuration. e Note 1: For the purpose of this AD, the thresholds and intervals, as defined in the ‘Compliance Time’ pages of the ALS, include specific compliance times for certain tasks. Corrective Action(s): p (2) In case of finding discrepancies during accomplishment of any task as required by paragraph (1) of this AD, before next flight, accomplish the applicable corrective action(s) in accordance with the applicable Airbus maintenance documentation. If a detected discrepancy cannot be corrected by using existing Airbus instructions, before next flight, contact Airbus for approved u instructions and accomplish those instructions accordingly. AD No.: 2025-0015 AMP Revision: (3) Within 12 months after the effective date of this AD, revise the AMP by incorporating the tasks and associated thresholds and intervals described in the ALS, as applicable to aeroplane model and depending on aeroplane configuration. Credit: (4) If, before the effective date of this AD, the AMP has been revised to incorporate the maintenance tasks as specified in a previous ALS revision, that action ensures the continued accomplishment of those tasks. d Consequently, for an aeroplane to which that AMP applies, it is acceptable to accomplish the new and/or more restrictive tasks, as defined in this AD, as applicable to aeroplane model and depending on aeroplane configuration, within the compliance times (see Note 1 of this AD) as e specified in the ALS to comply with paragraph (1) of this AD. For that AMP, it is acceptable to incorporate the new and/or more restrictive tasks, as specified in the ALS, as applicable to aeroplane model and depending on aeroplane configuration, into the AMP to comply with paragraph (3) of this AD. d Recording AD Compliance: (5) When the AMP of an aeroplane has been revised as required by paragraph (3) or (4) of this AD, as applicable, that action ensures continued accomplishment of the tasks as required by e paragraphs (1) and (2) of this AD for that aeroplane. Consequently, after revising the AMP, as required by paragraph (3) or (4) of this AD, as applicable, it is not necessary that accomplishment of individual action is recorded for demonstration of AD compliance on a continued basis. s
