AD No.: 2023-0138 The AMP: The Aircraft Maintenance Programme (AMP) contains the tasks on the basis of which the scheduled maintenance is conducted to ensure the continuing airworthiness of each operated aeroplane. For aeroplanes operated under EU regulation the operator or the owner ensures compliance with the AMP as stipulated in Commission Regulation (EU) 1321/2014. New and/or more restrictive tasks: This includes all tasks that are new and all tasks for which a threshold or interval was reduced, which were introduced through the Variation (as defined in this AD) since the previous ALS Revision that is currently incorporated in the AMP.

Reason: The airworthiness limitations and certification maintenance instructions for the Airbus A320 family aeroplanes, which are approved by EASA, are currently defined and published in the A318/A319/A320/A321 ALS documents. The airworthiness limitations and maintenance tasks D regarding to CMR are specified in Airbus A318/A319/A320/A321 ALS CMR Part 3. These instructions have been identified as mandatory for continued airworthiness. E Failure to accomplish these instructions could result in an unsafe condition. EASA previously issued AD 2022-0091, requiring the actions described in Airbus A318/A319/A320/A321 ALS Part 3 CMR Revision 08. D Since that AD was issued, Airbus published the Variation, as defined in this AD, which contains new and/or more restrictive tasks. E For the reason described above, this AD requires accomplishment of the actions specified in the Variation. EASA AD 2022-0091 is not superseded by this AD. S

Required Action(s) and Compliance Time(s): Required as indicated, unless accomplished previously: R Maintenance Tasks: (1) From the effective date of this AD, within the thresholds and intervals (see Note 1 of this AD), E accomplish all applicable maintenance tasks as specified in the Variation, as applicable to aeroplane model and depending on aeroplane configuration. P Where this AD requires a task which is already required per EASA AD 2022-0091, the instructions of the Variation invalidate the instructions of Airbus A318/A319/A320/A321 ALS Part 3 CMR Revision 08. U Note 1: For the purpose of this AD, the thresholds and intervals as defined in the ‘Compliance Time’ pages of the ALS include specific compliance times for certain tasks. S Corrective Action(s): (2) In case of finding discrepancies during accomplishment of any task as required by paragraph (1) of this AD, before next flight, accomplish the applicable corrective action(s) in accordance with the applicable Airbus maintenance documentation.
