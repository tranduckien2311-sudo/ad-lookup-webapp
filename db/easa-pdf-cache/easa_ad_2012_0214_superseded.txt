Supersedure: This AD supersedes DGAC France AD 2002-456(B)R1 dated 28 May 2003. E Airborne Auxiliary Power – Auxiliary Power Unit Exhaust Tailpipe –

ATA 49 Modification S

Manufacturer(s): Airbus (formerly Airbus Industrie)

Applicability: Airbus A318-111, A318-112, A318-121, A318-122, A319-111, A319-112, A319-113, A319-114, A319-115, A319-131, A319-132, A319-133, A320-111, R A320-211, A320-212, A320-214, A320-215, A320-216, A320-231, A320-232, A320-233, A321-111, A321-112, A321-131, A321-211, A321-212, A321-213, A321-231 and A321-232 aeroplanes, all manufacturer serial numbers. E

Reason: Until 2002, several cases had been reported of in-flight loss of an exhaust tailpipe from an APIC APS3200 Auxiliary Power Unit (APU), Part Number (P/N) 4500001, installed on A320 family aeroplanes. None of these occurrences resulted in any damage, either to the aeroplane, or to the area around the APU. Further analysis showed that cracks in the tailpipe inner liner were the cause of P detachment of the part. The affected APU’s have a lightweight tailpipe installed, either during APU manufacture, or through in-service modification of the APU. This condition, if not corrected, could lead to more cases of in-flight loss of the U exhaust tailpipe, possibly resulting in injury to persons on the ground. To address this unsafe condition, DGAC France issued AD 2002-456 (later revised) to require modification of the exhaust tailpipes of the affected APIC APS3200 P/N 4500001 APU’s. After that AD was issued, at least one A318 S aeroplane has been identified where an affected APU was installed. For the reasons described above, this AD retains the requirements of DGAC France AD 2002-456(B)R1, which is superseded, and expands the applicability to add A318 series aeroplanes.
