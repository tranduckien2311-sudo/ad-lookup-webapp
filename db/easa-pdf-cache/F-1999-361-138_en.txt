AIRBUS INDUSTRIE A319/A320/A321 aircraft LITTON GPSSU (ATA 34) APPLICABILITY: AIRBUS INDUSTRIE A319/A320/A321 all certified models, all serial numbers, equipped with LITTON GPSSU P/N 465205-0302-0303 installed by AIRBUS INDUSTRIE modification 23885 or AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Bulletin Service A320-34-1119, which have not received AIRBUS INDUSTRIE modification 28578 or AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-34-1191 or modification AIRBUS INDUSTRIE 28579 or AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-34-1196. REASON: Bad oscillator warm up characteristics may cause GPS satellite signals to be incorrectly measured by the GPSSU leading to position and altitude errors. This erroneous GPS position computation is not detected when there is only one GPS operative. ACTIONS: 1 - At the effective date of this

Airworthiness Directive, apply the following: A) Operation: - GPS stand-alone and GPS overlay Non Precision Approaches are not allowed, - the GPS must be deselected before Non precision Approchoach, - the GPS must be deselected for the remainder of the flight if "NAV FM/GPSPOS DISAGREE" ECAM warning is triggered (whatever the phase of the flight). Insert in Aircraft Flight Manual Temporary Revision (TR) 2.05.00/40 DGAC approved on July 30, 1999. B) Dispatch - If one GPS is inoperative, GPS must be deselected. 2 - Terminating action st - By not later than October 31 , 1999, unless already accomplished, install modified GPSSU in accordance with AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-34-1191 or AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-34-1196. d/GH … / … AIRBUS INDUSTRIE September 08, 1999 1999-361-138(B) A319/A320/A321aircraft
