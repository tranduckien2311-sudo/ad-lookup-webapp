Airworthiness Directive applies to AIRBUS INDUSTRIE A330 aircraft models -301; -321; -322; -341 and -342 which have not received the AIRBUS INDUSTRIE modifications 42724 or AIRBUS INDUSTRIE modification 43709, or AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A330-27-3034. In order to prevent any external hydraulic leakage of one of these spoiler servo-controls due to a loosening of the inlet filter which can lead to the loss of the associated hydraulic circuit, the following measures are rendered mandatory from the effective date of this

Airworthiness Directive, unless accomplished previously. - Within 18 months from the effective date of this

Airworthiness Directive, replace the inlet filter in each of the 12 spoiler servo-controls located at surface position 1 to 6 (LH and RH) and secure with lockwire in accordance with the instructions given in AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A330-27-3034. Ref. : AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A330-27-3034 EFFECTIVE DATE : JULY 29, 1995 d/JYR AIRBUS INDUSTRIE July 19, 1995 95-149-015(B) A330 Aircraft
