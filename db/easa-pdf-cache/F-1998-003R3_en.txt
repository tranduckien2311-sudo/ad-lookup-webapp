Airworthiness Directive. Translation of ‘Consigne de Navigabilité’ ref. : 1998-003(B) R3 In case of any difficulty, reference should be made to the French original issue. CFM INTERNATIONAL CFM56-5B turbofan engines Life limit of the low pressure turbine casing (ATA 05, 72) 1. APPLICABILITY: This

Airworthiness Directive applies to CFMI engines CFM56-5B1/2P, -5B2/2P, -5B3/2P, -5B4/2P installed on AIRBUS A319, A320, A321 or other aeroplanes. 2. REASONS: Results of a refined life analysis performed by the manufacturer have prompted life limit reduction for following parts, on a sub-population of 36 LPT casings P/N 338-117-404-0. Revision 1 and Revision 2 were issued to precise Part Numbers. This Revision 3 is issued to enable a life increase if compliance with SB CFM56-5B 72-229 prior a certain number of cycles. 3. MANDATORY ACTIONS: 3.1. New life limit if compliance with Service Bulletin CFM56-5B 72-229 prior to 6,000 cycles since new: LPT case P/N 338-117-404-0 on CFM56-5B2/2P and -5B3/2P is limited to 30,500 cycles. 3.2. New life limit if compliance with SB CFM56-5B 72-229 prior to 9,700 cycles since new: LPT case P/N 338-117-404-0 on CFM56-5B1/2P and -5B4/2P is limited to 30,500 cycles. 3.3. New life limit in other situations: a) LPT case P/N 338-117-404-0 on CFM56-5B2/2P and -5B3/2P is limited to 10,500 cycles. b) LPT case P/N 338-117-404-0 on CFM56-5B1/2P and -5B4/2P is limited to 15,500 cycles. n/JFS …/… CFM INTERNATIONAL December 12, 2001 1998-003(B) R3 CFM56-5B turbofan engines
