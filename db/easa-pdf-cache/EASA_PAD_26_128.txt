EASA PAD No.: 26-128 Reason: An investigation has revealed that safety cable brackets on the OWS, which can be installed on the left-hand or right-hand side of the helicopter, were fitted with standard fasteners, which are not tested to required quality standards. Their failure could cause detachment of the safety cable. This condition, if not corrected, could lead to detachment of the safety cable, potentially resulting in the operator falling from the helicopter. To address this unsafe condition, AH issued the ASB, to provide instructions for removal of the affected parts. For the reason described above, this AD requires removal of those affected parts. Required Action(s) and Compliance Time(s): Required as indicated by this AD, unless the action(s) required by this AD have been already accomplished: Removal: (1) For Group 1 helicopters: Within 250 flight hours or 6 months, whichever occurs first after the effective date of this AD, remove each affected part in accordance with the instructions of the ASB (see Note 1 of this AD). Note 1: Removal of the brackets as required by paragraph (1) of this AD includes removal of the related safety cable, fixation rails, connecting hardware and placards. Part(s) Installation: (2) For Group 1 and Group 2 helicopters: From the effective date of this AD, do not install an affected part on any helicopter. Ref. Publications: AH ASB MBB-BK117-25-23-0004 original issue (Issue 001) dated 15 October 2026. The use of later approved revisions of the above-mentioned document is acceptable for compliance with the requirements of this AD. Remarks: 1.
