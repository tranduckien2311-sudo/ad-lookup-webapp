ATA 53 – Fuselage – Fuselage Bulk Cargo Door Frames – Inspection / Repair s

Manufacturer(s): r Airbus (formerly Airbus Industrie) e

Applicability: Airbus A330-201, A330-202, A330-203, A330-223, A330-223F, A330-243, A330-243F, A330-301, A330-302, A330-303, A330-321, A330-322, A330-323, A330-341, A330-342 and A330-343 p aeroplanes, and Airbus A340-313, A340-541, A340-542, A340-642 and A340-643 aeroplanes, u manufacturer serial number (MSN) 0400 and higher.

Reason: S In the frame of the certification of the A330 Extended Service Goal exercise, it has been identified that Tartaric Sulfuric Anodising (TSA) / Chromic Acid Anodising (CAA) surface treatment is present in some frame holes, from aeroplane MSN 0400 and later MSN, following production process modification. On bulk cargo door frames (FR) 67 and FR 69 Right Hand Side, the door fitting attachment holes have this TSA / CAA treatment, which leads to a detrimental effect on fatigue behaviour. AD No.: 2016-0102 This condition, if not detected and corrected, could lead to critical cracks in the primary structure, possibly resulting in in-flight loss of a bulk cargo door, consequent decompression and potential damage to the aeroplane that could reduce the control of the aeroplane. To address this potential unsafe condition, Airbus issued Alert Operators Transmission (AOT) A53L012-16 to provide instructions to inspect the fuselage bulk cargo door frames at specific locations. For the reasons described above, this AD requires repetitive non-destructive test (rototest and high-frequency eddy-current (HFEC)) inspection or visual detailed (DET) inspections of the affected areas, and, depending on findings, accomplishment of a repair. This AD is considered an interim measure, and further AD action may follow. This AD is republished to correct the Applicability, where the Model A340-313 had inadvertently d been omitted.

Required Action(s) and Compliance Time(s): e Required as indicated, unless accomplished previously: d (1) Within the compliance times specified in Table 1 of this AD, accomplish a rototest inspection of the holes for the bulk cargo door support fittings at FR 67 and FR 69 and an HFEC inspection of the holes for the door latch fitting at FR 69, or a DET inspection of both same areas (bulk cargo e door support fittings at FR 67 and FR 69 and door latch fitting at FR 69), in accordance with the instructions of Airbus AOT A53L012-16. s Table 1 – Initial Inspection r Flight Cycles (FC) accumulated since aeroplane Compliance time first flight, on the effective date of this AD e 12 500 or more Within 200 FC or 2 months, whichever occurs first after the effective date of this AD p Less than 12 500 Within 200 FC or 2 months, whichever occurs first after exceeding 12 500 FC u (2) Following the initial inspection as required by paragraph (1) of this AD, at intervals not to exceed the value as specified in Table 2 of this AD, as applicable, depending on the selected S previous inspection method, inspect the holes for the bulk cargo door support fittings at FR 67 and FR 69 and the holes for the door latch fitting at FR 69, in accordance with the instructions of Airbus AOT A53L012-16.
