Effective Date: 15 December 2009 P Required action(s) Required as indicated : and Compliance U (1) Unless accomplished previously, perform a special detailed inspection Time(s): of elevator upper and lower skin panels , in accordance with the S instructions given in Airbus Service Bulletin (SB) A330-55-3039 or SB A340-55-4035, as applicable : a) For elevators that have not been inspected in accordance with Airbus SB A330-55-3032 or A340-55-4029 : Within 12 years (144 months) since the date of the elevator first flight on an aeroplane, or within 24 months after the effective date of this AD, whichever occurs later. b) For elevators that have been inspected in accordance with Airbus SB A330-55-3032 or A340-55-4029 : Within 6 years (72 months) since the date of the elevator first flight after the accomplishment of Airbus SB A330-55-3032 or A340-55-4029, or within 24 months after the effective date of this AD, whichever occurs later.
