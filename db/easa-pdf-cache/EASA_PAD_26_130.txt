EASA PAD No.: 26-130 The SB: CFM International (CFM) Service Bulletin (SB) CFM56-7B S/B 72-1119 original issue. Reason: An occurrence was reported of a fan blade failure on a CFM56-7B engine. The released fan blade was initially contained by the engine case, but there was subsequent uncontained forward release of debris. Preliminary investigation determined that the event was due to a fracture in the blade, which had initiated from the fan blade dovetail. This condition, if not detected and corrected, could lead to fan blade failure, possibly resulting in uncontained forward release of debris, with consequent damage to the engine and aeroplane. To address this potential unsafe condition, CFM International issued the SB, as defined in this AD, providing visual inspection and, in case of findings, corrective action instructions. For the reasons described above, this AD requires accomplishment of a one-time visual inspection and, depending on findings, corrective action(s). Required Action(s) and Compliance Time(s): Required as indicated by this AD, unless the action(s) required by this AD have been already accomplished: Inspection(s) for on-wing engines: (1) Within 400 flight cycles (FC) or within 90 days after the effective date of this AD, whichever occurs first, accomplish a visual inspection of each Fan Blade in accordance with the instructions of the SB. (2) From the effective date of this AD, installation of an engine on an aeroplane is allowed, provided that before next flight after installation or concurrently with the installation, as applicable, each Fan Blade is inspected in accordance with the instructions of the SB. Corrective Action(s): (3) If, during the inspection as required by paragraph (1) or (2) of this AD, as applicable, any discrepancy, as defined in the SB, is detected on a Fan Blade, within the compliance time specified in the SB, accomplish the applicable corrective action(s) in accordance with the instructions of the SB. Reporting: (4) If, during the inspection as required by paragraph (1) or (2) of this AD, as applicable, any discrepancy, as defined in the SB, is detected on a Fan Blade, within 30 days after that inspection, as applicable, or after the effective date of this AD, whichever occurs later, report the inspection results to CFM. The SB provides instructions which are acceptable to comply with this requirement.
