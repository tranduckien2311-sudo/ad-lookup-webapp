Airworthiness Directive. Translation of ‘Consigne de Navigabilité’ ref. : 94-168-058(B) R1 In case of any difficulty, reference should be made to the French original issue. AIRBUS INDUSTRIE A320 Aircraft Smoke detection in lavatories This

Airworthiness Directive applies to AIRBUS INDUSTRIE A320 aircraft, all models, without AIRBUS INDUSTRIE modification 22561 or without AIRBUS INDUSTRIE Service Bulletin A320-26-1017. In order to detect incorrect installation of the lavatory air extraction duct grill, which could reduce the performance of the smoke detection system, the following measures are rendered mandatory on July 30, 1994. 1- Within 450 flight hours, unless already accomplished, carry out a one time check, on each lavatory, for correct installation of the air extraction duct grille in accordance with the instructions of A.O.T. AIRBUS INDUSTRIE A320/AOT 26-12 dated June 06, 1994. 2- The application of

Airworthiness Directive 96-007-073(B) cancels requirements of this

Airworthiness Directive. Ref. : A.O.T. AIRBUS INDUSTRIE A320/AOT 26-12 dated June 06, 1994
