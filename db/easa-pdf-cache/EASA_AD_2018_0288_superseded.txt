Reason: The airworthiness limitations for the Airbus A320 family aeroplanes, which are approved by EASA, are currently defined and published in the A318/A319/A320/A321 ALS document(s). The Damage Tolerant (DT) Airworthiness Limitation Items (ALI) are published in ALS Part 2. Failure to accomplish these instructions could result in an unsafe condition. Previously, EASA issued AD 2017-0231 to require accomplishment of all DT ALI maintenance tasks as described in ALS Part 2 at Revision 06. Since that AD was issued, Airbus published the ALS, including new and/or more restrictive requirements, and new A321 models were certified and added to the Applicability. D For the reason described above, this AD retains the requirements of EASA AD 2017-0231, which is superseded, expands the Applicability and requires accomplishment of the actions specified in the ALS. E

Required Action(s) and Compliance Time(s): Required as indicated, unless accomplished previously: D Maintenance Tasks and Replacement of Life Limited Parts : (1) From the effective date of this AD, within the thresholds and intervals as defined in the ALS (see Notes 1 and 2 of this AD), accomplish all maintenance tasks as specified in the ALS, as E applicable to aeroplane model and depending on aeroplane configuration. Note 1: For the purpose of this AD, the thresholds and intervals as defined in the ‘Compliance Time’ S pages of the ALS include specific compliance times for certain tasks. Note 2: For the actions specified in task 531103-01-2 and task 531103-01-3, as defined in the ALS R Part 2 at Revision 07, additional requirements have been published in EASA AD 2018-0289. Corrective Action(s): (2) In case of finding discrepancies during accomplishment of any task as required by paragraph (1) E of this AD, before next flight, accomplish the applicable corrective action(s) in accordance with the applicable Airbus maintenance documentation. If a detected discrepancy cannot be corrected by using existing Airbus instructions, before next flight, contact Airbus for approved P instructions and accomplish those instructions accordingly. Aircraft Maintenance Programme (AMP) Revision: U (3) Within 12 months after the effective date of this AD, revise the approved AMP, on the basis of which the operator or the owner ensures the continuing airworthiness of each operated aeroplane, by incorporating the tasks and associated thresholds and intervals described in the S ALS, as applicable to aeroplane model and depending on aeroplane configuration. Consequently, for an aeroplane to which that AMP applies, it is acceptable to accomplish the new and more restrictive tasks (see Note 3 of this AD), as applicable to aeroplane model and depending on aeroplane configuration, as defined in, and within the compliance times (see Note 1 of this AD) as specified in, the ALS, to comply with paragraph (1) of this AD. D Note 3: For the purpose of this AD, ‘the new and more restrictive tasks’ include all tasks that have been introduced, and all tasks for which threshold and/or interval has been reduced in the ALS since E the previous revision that is currently incorporated in the AMP. Recording AD compliance: D (5) When the AMP of an aeroplane has been revised as required by paragraph (3) or (4) of this AD, as applicable, that action ensures (see Note 4 of this AD) continued accomplishment of the tasks as required by paragraphs (1) and (2) of this AD for that aeroplane. Consequently, after revising the AMP, as required by paragraph (3) or (4) of this AD, as applicable, it is not necessary E that accomplishment of individual action is recorded for demonstration of AD compliance on a continued basis.
