EASA AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE AD No : 2006-0047 Date: 16 February 2006 No person may operate an aircraft to which an

Airworthiness Directive applies, except in accordance with the requirements of that

Airworthiness Directive unless otherwise agreed with the Authority of the State of Registry. D Type Approval Holder’s Name : Type/Model designation(s) : AIRBUS SAS AIRBUS A330 aircraft E TCDS Number : EASA A.004 Foreign AD : NONE D Supersedure : DGAC AD F-2004-140(B) E Equipment/Furnishings – Inspection and modification of cockpit

ATA 25 instrument panel S

Manufacturer(s): AIRBUS SAS, AIRBUS INDUSTRIE

Applicability: AIRBUS aircraft A330, all certified models, all serial numbers, except those on R which AIRBUS modification 53446 has been embodied in production or AIRBUS Service Bulletin (SB) A330-25-3249 has been embodied in service.

Reason: One A330 operator has reported damage of a bracket PN F2511012920000, E which is one of the 8 parts used to attach the cockpit instrument panel to the aircraft structure. This bracket has been found cracked on two aircraft and, in one case, both vertical flanges of this bracket were found completely broken. P Investigations have revealed that the damage is a bending crack caused by the assembly procedure (tightening of the bracket), combined with lateral load introduced by differential pressure and inertial effects. This hidden failure, breaking of this bracket, combined with failure of horizontal U beam, can lead to collapse the left part of the cockpit panel which could result in the worst case in a reduced controllability of the aircraft. In order to prevent the risk of having the bracket fully broken, and further damage to the surrounding structure, the

Airworthiness Directive (AD) F- S 2004-140 required a mandatory repetitive detailed visual inspection of the affected bracket. This new AD: 1/4

Effective Date: 01 March 2006 Compliance: 1. INSPECTION: The following measures are rendered mandatory from August 28, 2004 D (effective date of the AD F-2004-140): 1.1. Unless already accomplished, before accumulation of 16 500 flight E cycles (FC), perform a detailed visual inspection of the LH bracket without removal of fasteners in accordance with instructions given in AIRBUS Service Bulletin (SB) A330-25-3227 Revision 01. D 1.2. If the two flanges of the bracket are fully broken, perform a detailed visual inspection of the horizontal beam in accordance with AIRBUS SB A330-25-3227 Revision 01: E • If crack is found on the horizontal beam, contact AIRBUS before next flight. • If no crack is found on the horizontal beam, apply AIRBUS SB A330- S 25-3249 before next flight. 1.3. If any crack is found on the bracket during the inspection defined in § 1.1.: R - before next flight, replace the affected bracket in accordance with instructions given in AIRBUS SB A330-25-3227 Revision 01, E - and perform the inspection defined in § 1.1 of the new installed bracket at a threshold of 16 500 FC from the last replacement, - and according to the results of this inspection, perform the actions P mentioned in § 1.2 or 1.3 or 1.4 of this AD. Note 1: AIRBUS recommends to replace the bracket by a new reinforced one in accordance with SB A330-25-3249 instructions before U reaching the second inspection threshold. 1.4. If no crack has been detected, repeat the inspection mentioned in § 1.1. S at intervals not exceeding 13 800 FC. 1.5. Report any cracked or broken bracket occurrence to AIRBUS. 2/4
